# Artwork, source and music

The MIT license covers the software. It does not automatically grant rights to the soundtrack, third-party trademarks or external source material.

`public/scene-assets/` and `public/scene-plans/` retain the project's working illustrations, AI-generated variants and visual plans so readers can understand the production. Where a neighboring prompt or provenance JSON exists, keep it with the asset. Missing provider/license records must not be interpreted as a universal commercial-use grant.

Third-party browser, social-network and AI logos identify technologies in the story. Their respective owners retain their rights. Replace them if your use requires different permissions.

Original song audio and twelve-channel stems remain outside this repository. Supply music you have rights to use. Before redistributing a new video, check the terms applicable to its generation/download and any included third-party assets.

For a new production, generate your own characters and scene artwork; retain prompts, sources, permitted uses and revision history. Do not infer endorsement by a company whose logo appears.

Public snapshots omit internal message identifiers and original workstation metadata paths. Source hashes and creative scene structure are retained. Historical approval fields describe this example only; they do not approve a new production.
