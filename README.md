## The idea

The Planet's Story follows information from home to home. Ep.1
is the first home: before writing existed, information lived in
living bodies — kept in the body itself (a spotted coat, copied
into the young) and in behaviour (a route to water, copied by
watching) — and both keepings died with any body that never
passed them on. The episode installs the series' engine: an
archive that can die is why information had to find other homes.

## The objective (5–20 words)

> **Teach that before writing, the first archive was a living
> body — and everything it kept died with it, uncopied.**
> (20 words)
