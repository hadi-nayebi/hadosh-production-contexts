## The question the video answers

**Where did information live before writing?** — asked by the
narration itself in line 1, guessed at during a designed
silence, and answered in full by the close: in living bodies —
and it died with them.

## Style — a recorded experiment

**The illustrated world, performed.** Spine: the lane's own
picture-book world (format v4) — painted plates, independent
cutouts, hand-lettered word animation in planned negative
space. **The variable under test: performed transformations**
inside held plates (the copy arriving; the route drawing
itself; the light dying; motion halting at the sting) —
attacking this piece's own recorded weakness (H7: states over
transformations).

Weighed and not chosen: the **A59 typographic void** (soa-01 /
wd-01 / wd-02's grammar — a definitional-lane stage; this
piece's proofs are animals, not terms) and **whiteboard
construction** (already under test on ws01-v2; a construction
register fights this story's warmth). **Hypothesis:** for a
story-lane episode, attention rides on a world that changes in
place; if the performed transformations hold the viewer
better than plate succession did, the lane gains its default
visual mode for the next batch.
