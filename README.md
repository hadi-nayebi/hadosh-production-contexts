## The idea

State of Adoption is a report lane: one dated, sourced fact per
short, read against the Academy's prescription — own your harness.
This piece reports the lane's founding fact: on September 29, 2026,
at OpenAI's DevDay, Sam Altman reported **1.2 billion weekly ChatGPT
users**. The idea is to let that number testify twice — first as
scale (adoption really is total), then as evidence (nearly every one
of those users rents the layer they use, and owns none of it).

## The objective (5–20 words)

> **Show 1.2 billion weekly users rent AI access while owning none
> of the layer around it.** (16 words)
