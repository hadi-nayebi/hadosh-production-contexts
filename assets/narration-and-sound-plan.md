**Silence** below means silence from designed layers while the locked narration remains audible. The initial void and the hold after the final voice can be true silence. Preserve the L06 reading breath, the L16 reflection, and the L18 question. Waveforms and translated examples do not introduce extra voices.

The map uses existing motionBeat titles. Each interval appears once, alone or in a contiguous range. Grouped rows sustain or withdraw one treatment; any distinct event inside the group is named. IDs, rather than provisional timestamps, are the synchronization references.

## Mix, timing and playback requirements

The voice leads. Keep designed layers out of intelligibility-critical consonants and dense factual phrases; reduce or remove them whenever they compete. Retain natural breaths. Use restrained spatial movement only when the image supplies a direction, with a centered narrator and a checked mono presentation. Audition the result on headphones and ordinary speakers. Loudness, peaks and playback level require measurement during mixing; no numerical level or compliance is claimed here.

Prefer one aligned playback master containing voice and sound, retaining editable stems for revision. Match the cue map to the accepted recording and update passage/beat performance times. Listen and watch every phrase and object boundary; passage retiming alone does not establish fine alignment.

Pause must stop all sound at the same playhead as the image. Seeking resumes the correct audio position without firing skipped events. Restart cancels prior playback before starting one new stream. Replay, rapid pause/restart, chapter jumps, mute, loading failure and duration mismatch require actual tests with the produced asset. No audio behavior is considered verified by this brief.
