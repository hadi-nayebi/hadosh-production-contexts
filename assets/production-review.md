## Review pass 1 — requirements

The review checked the requested entry surface, locked material, four-part structure, new visual production and recorded plan.

The film opens into the motion canvas with pause-state controls, rather than a production dashboard. The opening begins with a void, introduces the concrete five-word sentence “The river overflowed its banks.”, reduces words to marks and resolves the complete title. Later passages retain language as a recurring object through processing, prediction and attention.

All four parts have implemented scenes. The stock/flow comparison, useful operations, history, model and external-work sequence are present. Later sections are not left as headings or empty storyboard slots. The P4 job sequence includes a read request, a brief, a draft change, a criterion check and a result returned toward a person.

The plan and transcript checks passed at source level. Visual coverage was established through the sampled renders. **The requirement for a finished audiovisual film is still open**, because the fresh voice, sound and final synchronization do not yet exist.

## Review pass 2 — common sense and usability

The review used contact sheets to locate weaknesses and original-size images to decide whether they were actual defects. Desktop and portrait were assessed as separate compositions.

This pass found several problems that source geometry checks had missed: a processing label over a stock tile, two words sharing one transition seat, two footer labels colliding, a recap competing with the end card, and a search operation too small and faint to explain its work clearly. Each finding was tied to a passage and timecode before repair.

The corrected L12 now retains a visible request and stages the source stack and answer at a readable size. The corrected portrait L07/L19/L20/L25 samples no longer contain the reported collisions. The larger P2 operations remain readable in both layouts, and the quiet retained workstation in L16 reads as a deliberate pause. P4's end card is readable after the transition completes.

These findings support the specific corrections. They are not a final creative grade for the whole film.

## Review pass 3 — critical verification

The review deliberately checked misleading implications, unequal visual units, unstable word layout, playback races, source boundaries and overstatements of completion.

The common comparison uses one equal-size amber cell against ten full blue cells plus a two-thirds remainder. The fractional cell clips the same internal pattern to its actual width. The monthly relationship is approximate; the separate illustrative pass markers do not claim to reproduce the monthly count. The source stock remains intact while representative words circulate.

The opening sentence and its particle source share one layout. Later recurring text reserves the final word's space, avoiding a prefix jump when the missing word appears. The audio-ready controller includes an asynchronous start guard and a recording-duration check; neither is presented as tested final audio while the narration asset is absent.

Actual interaction testing also exposed a control defect that source review had not caught: pause-state UI updates could overwrite a new chapter or typed-time value before the change handler used it. The handlers were corrected to capture the value first, and numeric entry received valid-input and Enter handling. Subsequent actual UI retests reached 111.5 seconds from numeric input and reached the correct part starts at 35.6, 81.2 and 121.5 seconds from the chapter selector. These are observed interaction results, separate from the earlier source review; the detailed behavior log belongs with the final build record.

## Playback measurement and its limit

Two visible-browser runs were recorded. The final complete run was made in a 1363×936 viewport. It reached 143 s, reported L25B07, paused, displayed Replay and recorded no renderer errors.

| Recorded run | Frames | Average drawing cost | Maximum drawing cost | Maximum callback gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Earlier 35.6–143 s sample, approximately 107.4 s elapsed | 209 | 1.24 ms | 10.8 ms | 1,016.5 ms |
| Final complete 0–143 s visual playhead run | 170 | 1.86 ms | 34.5 ms | 1,016.6 ms |

Those drawing costs describe work performed when a frame was delivered. They do not explain or excuse the long gaps between delivered frames. The surface provided sparse animation callbacks even while visible. The evidence does not establish whether the cause was in the film, browser, hosting surface or surrounding execution conditions.

**No smoothness or frame-rate certification is made.** Final motion quality requires continuous review on the intended playback surface, including the corrected transitions.

The subsequent Replay retest returned to **0.009 s**, with playback active, controls inert and no renderer errors. This establishes observed end-state recovery for the visual edition; it does not establish audio synchronization.
