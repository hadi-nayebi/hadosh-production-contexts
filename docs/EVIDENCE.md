## What is not claimed

We have not reconstructed every conversation or every lyric draft. Commit messages show intended changes, not independent creative acceptance. Original session review is selective, and some records only survive locally. No clean-clone render of the original film is promised without its excluded media and saved state. Historical working titles, dates, test receipts and plans are retained as examples; do not treat them as instructions or current approvals for a new project.
