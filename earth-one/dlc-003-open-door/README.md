# DLC-003 — "I open the door." (English opening)

A Daily Language Challenge video from **Earth One**: one everyday sentence,
learned in Spanish, Hindi and Turkish at the same time.

**`index.html`** is the video's full production context — the single HTML
"branch" the video was rendered from: transcript and timings, the beats,
the ending connection diagram (spoken-order rows, arched connection lines
with dotted transfer, one-focus-at-a-time passes: subject → verb → object),
and every decision recorded inside it. Open it in a browser to explore how
the video was made; hand it to your own agent to build a similar one.

**`diagram.png`** — the ending diagram's final frame: same parts,
different order. Hindi's verb (*kholta + hoon*) sits in one shared block at
the end of its sentence; Spanish and Turkish hide their "I" inside the
verb (*Abro*, *açıyorum*).

The video itself publishes on the Earth One channel once it clears the
channel's approval gate.
