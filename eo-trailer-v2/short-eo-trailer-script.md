## Narrative declaration (N-04 — written BEFORE the draft)
- **Structure: the Channel Map** (catalogue beat-sheet). ORIENT
  (what this place is) → SHOW ONE (the planet's story, compressed
  to its arc) → SHOW TWO (words in 5 languages) → PROMISE (plain
  statement) → MAKING (the brand beat) → FAMILY (relationship
  line) → ASK (spoken CTA). A trailer's job is navigation, not a
  single teaching arc — the map IS the structure, declared here
  before drafting per N-04.
- **Intended feeling:** clarity and welcome — a cold viewer should
  feel "I understand this place, and I know where to start"
  within 45 seconds.
- **Villain (N-06), named: the channel that explains nothing.**
  The enemy is Confusion: an endless feed of videos that leave
  you with nothing you can say back. Stacked consequences:
  (1) you watch and retain nothing → (2) you cannot tell what a
  channel is FOR, so you never subscribe → (3) good explainers
  drown in noise. Earth One's whole format (one clear idea,
  plainly stated, sayable-back) is the counter-move; the trailer
  names the promise, not the villain — the villain is the design
  target, stated here per N-06.
- **Most vivid detail, chosen on purpose (N-03 tyranny rule):**
  the red hand pressed on the cave wall — one image that has
  been speaking for tens of thousands of years. Held on screen
  during the "marks" beat (L4) so the trailer's most memorable
  picture is the series' own thesis: information outliving its
  maker.
- **Chosen time anchor:** none needed — the trailer is a map,
  not an episode; the arc beats (bodies → stories → marks →
  songs → print) carry the timeline in compressed form.

information evolved on this planet, and words taught in five

time."

## Iteration log

### Fix cycle v2 (2026-10-23) — the name-context rewrite

**DR-L8B — every name carries its gloss in the same breath
(Hadi's name-drop ruling, 2026-10-23 → QC T-18, checklist v2.12).**
- **Choice:** v1's single family line is replaced by two lines.
  L8: "Earth One grows out of Observations — a written series
  from Hadosh Academy, a project teaching people to build their
  own software with AI." L9 (new): "That teaching is on video
  too — on a sister YouTube channel, Let's Build It Together."
  Every other line is verbatim from v1; the CTA follows as L10.
  Each name is glossed in its own breath: Observations = a
  written series; Hadosh Academy = a project teaching people to
  build their own software with AI; Let's Build It Together = a
  sister YouTube channel where that teaching is on video.
- **Alternatives considered:** (a) keep one line and compress
  the glosses into it — the single breath ran past 35 words and
  buried the channel name at the end of a subordinate pile-up;
  the split gives the channel its own breath and its own beat.
  (b) Drop the names and say "our written series" / "our other
  channel" — rejected: the names ARE the routing (a viewer must
  be able to search the channel name); T-18 asks for a gloss,
  not an erasure. (c) v1's wording — failed Hadi's gate.
- **Context it serves:** Hadi's verdict on v1 (2026-10-23): the
  trailer is "not bad," but toward the end it names the Academy
  and the channel with no context — "you just drop it like that
  without mentioning that a sister channel or something…
  name dropping without context is just confusing… the content
  has a lot of dependencies and prior understanding… it is not
  a standalone content." Codified the same day as QC T-18
  (checklist v2.12): no name without its context in the same
  breath, in fresh words (anti-signature law applies to the
  gloss too — "sister channel" is used because it is the exact
  relationship word Hadi himself reached for, not a stock
  phrase carried from another piece).
- **Why this one:** the two-line split keeps each gloss inside
  the breath of its name, preserves the Observations door
  (DR-L8's doctrine: the Academy connection runs through the
  series, and the teaching identity stays with the flagship),
  and lands the channel name as the family's closing beat —
  the last new information before the ask.
- **What would change it:** Hadi re-framing the Earth One ↔
  Academy relationship, or a measured cold-viewer test showing
  a gloss still doesn't land.
