**Hadi**

3.2 quadrillion tokens processed every month. And that is Google alone, by Google’s own count. Its monthly processing grew by more than 300 times in two years.

To understand the scale, consider this. The usable public text written by people is estimated at 300 trillion tokens. That’s about a tenth of one month at Google. Turn it around. In a month, Google processes over ten times that estimate. The same words, passing through again and again.

## Unit Two

And here’s what it does. Every token of it goes to work. A search comes back as a written answer. A voice becomes text. A long document, a summary. Words written in one language are read in another.

If the electron stream was a general form of energy, the token stream is a general form of intelligence.

If the Industrial Revolution multiplied humanity’s physical power, this could be the start of an era that multiplies humanity’s cognitive power.

## Unit Three

What set this in motion? The trail leads back to a paper from 2017, *Attention Is All You Need*.

By then, building AI models that could understand and translate language had long been a holy grail. Learning to predict the next word offered a way into the patterns of language. So a team at Google built a new architecture around attention. It could learn which earlier words mattered and pay more attention to them when predicting the next word.

The Transformer was born. A language model is formed through training on vast collections of text.
