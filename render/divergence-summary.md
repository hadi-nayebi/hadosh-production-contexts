**On the convergence.** The definition sentence, the
machine's-eye contrast and the performed count read closely to
the first draft's teaching lines — because they ARE the piece's
settled teaching, stable since the first build and never
faulted by the owner, and because Candidate B (this morning's
redevelopment, built under the owner's structure direction)
re-derived them and this consolidation re-checked the
derivation line by line against a fresh candidate
(consolidation §§4–5). The owner's verdict on the first draft
was aimed at its structure and style — "absolutely zero style
in this video as an explainer", scored "10 at most" — and that
is exactly where this build diverges completely: no shared
hook, no shared structure, no shared close, no shared scene,
no shared delivery, no shared voice. A rebuild identical to
its first draft would have failed; a rebuild that churned the
settled definition to manufacture difference would have failed
differently. This one diverges where the verdict pointed.
