## Video A — the clone voice

**Title:** Who Owns Your Layer? — 1.2 Billion Renters (Voice: the Creator's, Cloned)

**File:** `render/soa-01-final-clone.mp4`

**Description:**

1.2 billion people use ChatGPT every week. Almost none of them
own the layer they build inside it — their instructions, their
memory, their files sit in a rented chat box.

This short names that layer — the harness — and asks who it
belongs to.

THE EXPERIMENT: this exact film exists twice. This version is
narrated in the creator's own voice, cloned. Its twin is
narrated in a stock AI voice. Same words, same pictures, same
music — only the voice differs. We published both to learn,
in the open, whether the voice changes what a video earns.
