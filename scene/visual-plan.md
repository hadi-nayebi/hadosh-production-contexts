## 1. How screenshots sit inside the scene's grammar

The v3 scene is type-led motion graphics in the house palette
(dark charcoal, cyan + amber). Screenshots must read as citizens
of that world, not pasted photos. Standing treatment for every
capture in this plan:

- **Glass plate:** the screenshot sits on a dark-charcoal glass
  panel with a 2 px cyan hairline frame, ~24 px rounded corners,
  and a soft inner shadow — the same plate language the Earth
  One scenes use for artwork, so flagship and Earth One stay
  one family.
- **Safe zone:** every plate lives inside V-14 (x 100–960,
  y 250–1500). Plate width ≈ 800 px, centred (x ≈ 140–940).
- **Focus, not clutter:** inside a plate, everything except the
  target region is dimmed to ~35% brightness. The target region
  stays full-brightness inside an **amber focus frame** — amber
  is the house "lit / being spoken" signal (v3 L9 already
  lights named rows amber; the focus frame is that same signal
  applied to a real page).
- **Zoom:** the target region is shown at a scale where its
  text is legible at phone size (URL/filename glyphs ≈ 28 px+
  on the 1080-wide frame). Verify each plate with a 180 px-wide
  legibility render, the S-09 habit.
- **One signal per frame (S-05):** the amber focus and a popped
  narration word never compete in the same instant — the focus
  draws on, settles, THEN the word pops; or the word band sits
  in the plate's empty margin while the focus holds.
- **Word animation placement (V-16):** narration words take
  the plate's negative space (the band above/below the plate
  inside the safe rect), never across the screenshot's own
  text. Build-time rect asserts cover plate + word bands
  together, as in the v3 template.
- **Component discipline (H-01):** each screenshot is its own
  scene component with a hover note and a Q-0 Decision Record
  compartment, embedded base64 (zero external references), and
  is a **replaceable asset** — the publish pass re-captures two
  of them (§5) without touching the scene's code.

## 3. The gap in the script — and the PROPOSED line

**Check against the doctrine's step (3):** the v4 script must
tell viewers, in words, that the best path is to point their own
agent at this context (via the description) and have it
customize the pattern for their own videos.

- L10 says: "Take it. Give it to your agent. Change it, and
  make your own." That covers *give it to your agent* and
  *change it* — by hand, in the reading a cold viewer most
  naturally takes.
- It never says **where** the context is for the viewer (the
  description, one tap below the video they are watching).
- It never says the **recommended path**: point your agent AT
  the context and let the agent do the customizing — which is
  the Academy's whole thesis (you instruct the agent; the agent
  works the files) and the one instruction that turns a curious
  viewer into a builder.

**Conclusion: no spoken line covers step (3).** The line below
is drafted in the script's own voice (short plain imperatives,
L10's cadence) and is **PROPOSED — it goes to Hadi's ear; the
script file is not edited by this plan.**

> **★ PROPOSED LINE (insert after L10):** "The link is in the
> description. Point your agent at it — and let it shape the
> whole pattern around your own videos."
> *(22 words)*

- **Placement:** inside the hand-over, after the take (L10) and
  before the thesis (L11). The thesis — "That is how we build
  here" — must remain the teaching arc's last word, and the
  Academy beat must still follow it (the draft's placement
  reasoning is position-relative: invitation after proof; the
  beat still lands after the thesis, renumbered L13–L14 if
  this line is adopted).
- **Runtime impact:** at the standing 0.45 s/word planning
  pace, 22 words ≈ **+9.9 s** → v4 total ≈ **81 s** (the
  draft projects ≈71 s without it). At the intro's own
  measured pace (0.51 s/word incl. gaps, per the draft) ≈
  +11.2 s → ≈82 s. Budgeted honestly: this line spends the
  piece's remaining S-03 slack; see the offset below.
- **Compact variant (if Hadi wants the pointer at half price):**
  "It is linked in the description. Point your agent at it —
  make it yours." *(14 words ≈ +6.3 s at 0.45 s/word → ≈77 s
  total.)* It keeps the where + the pointing, drops the
  explicit "customize it for your own videos" clause.
- **Pre-logged offset:** the draft already names L7 as the
  cut candidate (−8 words ≈ −3.6 s). Proposed line + L7 cut ≈
  77 s (full) / 74 s (compact). The choice among {no line,
  compact, full, full + L7 cut} is Hadi's, with these numbers
  in front of him.
- **Design note:** the proposed line speaks no URL — "the
  description" in plain words — so it adds no TTS-rendering
  risk (contrast L13's spoken address, draft production
  note 2). The link itself is carried visually by CAP-DESC at
  the same moment (SAID = SHOWN in both directions).
