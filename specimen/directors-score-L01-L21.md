## §3 — MOVEMENT GRAMMAR (shared with the Academy film — reused, not re-derived)

Named curves (cubic-bezier control points / definitions identical to
the Academy scores' §Movement Grammar):

| Profile | Curve | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **TRAVEL** | (0.42, 0, 0.58, 1) | rest → rest; peak velocity ≈ 1.5 × average at midpoint |

Velocity convention: **v_avg = path length ÷ duration**, stated as a
number for every travel; endpoint velocities are the named curve's
own (TRAVEL/SETTLE end at 0 px/s; LAUNCH starts at 0 px/s).
**Opacity:** α changes use SETTLE (in) / LAUNCH (out) unless marked
LINEAR (scene crossfades + form crossfades) or SWELL (pulses).
**Idle grammar (shared):** nodes breathe — scale 1 ± 0.008 SWELL,
period 5.0 + 0.7 × slot-index s; links carry a shimmer front at
**120 px/s CONST**, one per link, phase-offset by slot; light points
at rest are perfectly still; the Model's halo breathes SWELL ± .05,
period 4.2 s; figures and architecture hold **decided stillness**
except where a row scores otherwise; **type never breathes**.
**Web traffic:** points travel links at 120 px/s CONST; at maturity
(L16 on) traffic slows to **75 px/s** — stated as speeds, never zero.
**Node births are condensations** (scale .35→1.0 + α 0→1, SETTLE,
with birth overshoot — scale peaks 1.05 at 85% of the window, settles
to 1.00); every birth breathes its ring (+5% radii, TRAVEL-value).

**Lens deviations from the shared grammar — decided here, with
reasons:** (a) the case-chips *float* (Scene L-B) — an unlinked layer
drifts because nothing organizes it; the drift is scored, not the
renderer defaulting; (b) the Watcher does **not** breathe — it sits
outside the specimen's physics; stillness is its scored idle;
(c) Earth does not rotate and its speckles barely shimmer (Scene
L-A) — the planet is a record, not a screensaver.
