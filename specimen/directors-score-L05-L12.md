## OWNER-1 RESOLUTION — 2026-10-28 — DECIDED (Archer; #109 closed by decision, not by Hadi's ruling)

**Decision: KEEP the contact** — the HARNESS tiles remain over the Model's lower rim in L8, exactly as staged and capped at Gate 1.

**Why this was never Hadi's question (his ask-triage ruling, 2026-10-28):** it passes neither gate 3 test — a craft standard exists (gate 1) and the context to judge it was in hand (gate 2). The re-audit's OWNER tag was a proposal; relaying it uncritically was the defect.

**Gate 1 — best practice (spatial/kinetic typography):** readability governs overlap, not overlap itself. Type treated as a physical element in frame, layered over subjects for depth, is standard practice; the binding constraints are: foreground type stays fully opaque with strong contrast (≥4.5:1), the subject's identity-bearing features stay unobscured, and every line gets a beat of full legibility, checked sound-on and sound-off. **Gate 2 — this instance against those constraints:** the tiles are cast CREAM on NAVY with GOLD outline (contrast strong, Gate-1 measured contact, tiles crisp on top); the contact is with the disc's lower RIM — a non-identifying edge; the Model's lit core and form read whole; the word is the line's semantic peak and crowns the thing it names at the spoken word "harness." Both alternatives degrade decided goods: B crowds the ring/link zone; C weakens the Model in its hero scene.

**Standing rule (from this decision):** type may overlap a subject's non-identifying edge when (1) the type stays fully legible at phone size, (2) the subject's identity features stay unobscured, (3) the overlap is semantically motivated, (4) the contact is measured and capped. Any condition failing = a defect Archer fixes (reposition / resize / re-time / redraw) without asking.
