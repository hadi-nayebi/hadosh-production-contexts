**How to read this file.** Lines are synthesized one per take.
**Marks govern intra-line performance only**; the silence AFTER each
line is an assembly gap from the locked map (lead 1.20 s · tail
2.40 s), listed per line with the map's own reason. Clean text is
quoted verbatim from the transcript of record — marks never change
a word. No line's clean or marked text contains the banned word
(the transcript's recorded scan is 0; the marks add none).

**Film-level decisions (recorded once, applied per line):**
- **The register is a memo, not an address.** The analyst states,
  describes, analyzes, recommends — he does not urge. Markup is
  therefore *sparser* than the Academy twin's by decision: caps is
  reserved for the count, the custody pivots, the differentiator
  pair, and the recommendation triad. Lines the memo merely reports
  are unmarked by decision, and say so.
- **Exclamation: foreclosed for this film.** An analyst's memo has
  no excitement beat; the recommendation's force is its calm.
- **Ellipsis: foreclosed for this film.** Its pauses are the locked
  map's architecture (above all the 1.60 s held pause after L18) and
  one measured intra-line breath (L19's sentence joint, 1.06 s in
  the take of record, produced by a period — kept a period). No beat
  is a hesitation.
- **The triad rule (L19–L21):** the imperative VERB carries the caps,
  as in the Academy twin — the score's ice-set type stack lands
  UNDERSTAND / BUILD / KEEP AN EYE as kept rows, and voice and type
  peak together.
