**Sources of intent, per line:** the transcript of record

revision notes), the Director's Scores' measured word anchors

**How to read this file.** Lines are synthesized one per take.
**Marks govern intra-line performance only**; the silence AFTER each
line is an assembly gap from the locked map (lead 1.20 s · tail
2.00 s), listed per line so the delivery record is complete in one
place. Clean text is quoted verbatim from the transcript of record —
marks never change a word. No line's clean or marked text contains
the banned word (the content text's recorded scan is 0; the marks
add none).

**Film-level decisions (recorded once, applied per line):**
- **Exclamation: foreclosed for this film.** No line's recorded
  intent is excitement or exhortation; the register is measured
  direct address. An "!" anywhere would be decoration.
- **Ellipsis: foreclosed for this film.** Its silences are
  architectural (the locked gap map, incl. the 1.2 s designed silence
  before L6) or measured breaths (L3's clause pause, L10's dashes) —
  no beat is a hesitation or a trail-off.
- **The custody system:** the film turns on yours/theirs, so the
  possessive pivot word carries the caps where a beat turns on
  custody (L2, L5, L10, L11, L16); payload nouns/verbs carry it
  elsewhere (L1, L3, L8, L9, L13, L14, L17, L18).
- **The triad rule (L19–L21):** the imperative VERB carries the caps
  in each line — the kinetic-type layer strikes exactly those verbs
  (score M-T2/M-T3), and voice, type, and word peak together.
