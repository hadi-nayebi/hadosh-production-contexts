## The challenge

## The honest audit
The shipped style (moody cinematic illustration; ink-navy base, gold light, cream highlights; paper/leather/lamplight workshop world) was chosen by the foundation job with this recorded reasoning: LINEAGE — it is the palette family of the trunk soa-01 v2, the version that scored 85 — plus a consistency mechanism (one style block, measured acceptance). That reasoning is real but answers the WRONG question: "what worked before," not "what fits THIS story." The story is about AI and computers; a software layer made of a person's own words, kept as files in directories. The workshop world argues "crafted and kept" but hides the domain — the visual twin of the transcript sin v4 fixed (hiding what the harness is). Style verdict: consistent, handsome, UNDERIVED. Randomness by inheritance.

## The fitted direction (derived from the story)
The imagery should be drawn from INFORMATION-KEEPING itself — the visual vocabulary of the thing the film is about:
- LETTERS as material: the layer is literally made of the user's words; scenes where letters/words are the building substance (streams of letters settling into a layer, a wall of type, words filed like cards).
- OLD-SCHOOL INFORMATION PATTERNS: index-card drawers and file cards, directory-tree branching, blueprint grids, punch-card dot patterns, ledger rulings — the patterned systems humans used to store and retrieve information before and at the dawn of computing. These read sound-off as "stored, organised, retrievable, YOURS."
- Keep the navy/gold cinematic base (it carries value/ownership and earned its scores) — re-motif the SUBJECTS, not the palette.
Why it helps delivery (information-density doctrine): the scenes stop being generic mood and start teaching — every stretch shows a facet of "your words, stored as files, in a structure you own."

## Adjusted style spec — CANDIDATE (2026-10-28, specimen job; for Hadi's judgment — does NOT replace scene-plan §2 unless he blesses it)

**Adjusted STYLE BLOCK** (exact string; one block used verbatim in every
adjusted prompt, same discipline as §2 — prompt = `[STYLE BLOCK] +
[subject sentence]`):

*"Moody cinematic illustration, deep ink-navy and near-black shadows,
warm gold practical light, cream highlights, soft volumetric light,
subtle film grain, built from old-school information-keeping motifs —
index-card catalogue drawers, file cards, directory-tree branching,
blueprint grids, punch-card dot patterns — letters and glyphs appear

can be read, one clear subject in the middle band, darker quiet
negative space in the top and bottom thirds, no readable text, no
words, no logos, no close-up faces, vertical 9:16."*

**Motif rules.**
- **M1 — Letters are material, never messages.** The layer is made of
  the user's words, so letterforms may appear as substance — streams
  of loose glyphs settling into order, type-texture on card faces,
  letters compressed into strata — but they must dissolve into
  pattern before resolving: no readable word, phrase, or sentence
  anywhere in any image. The word layer (§7) owns every readable
  word in the film; an image that spells something steals its job
  and breaks the no-text rule.
- **M2 — Storage systems are the scenery.** Subjects are built from
  the pre-digital/computing-dawn systems for keeping information:
  index-card drawers and file cards (accumulation, retrieval),
  directory-tree branching (a structure that is yours, that grows),
  blueprint grids (a designed, inspectable plan), punch-card dot
  patterns (information as physical pattern), ledger/card strata
  (distillation). Every motif must read sound-off as *stored,
  organised, retrievable*.
- **M3 — One subject, middle band, dark thirds** (unchanged from §2):
  the word layer still lives in the top/bottom negative space; the
  motif density stays in the middle band so scenes never compete
  with the type.
- **M4 — Marks carry correction, glyphs never do.** Where a beat
  involves feedback (S13), the S13-repair clarification governs
  unchanged: red check marks, underlines, strike strokes — marks
  only, never letterforms.
- **M5 — Palette continuity.** Ink-navy base, gold practical light,
  cream highlights, painterly cinematic rendering — identical to §2.
  Only the subjects' material changes (paper/leather/lamp →
  card/grid/punch-pattern), so the candidate can be judged against
  the shipped style on motif alone, and can roll across the film
  without re-grading the piece.

**Reasoning, rule → story.** The film argues that a harness is a
