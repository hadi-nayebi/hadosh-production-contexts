## The weighing — joint by joint

**The hook (L1).** A compressed its form ("Writing is brand
new — five and a half thousand years. Before it, where did
information live?"). B's form states the number inside a plain
sentence, then asks. A's opener spends its first word on a
judgment ("brand new") the number already makes; B's "only"
does the same work inside the fact. **B's form settles** —
which is i7's sentence with C1's corrected number. The
convergence is on a fact-corrected, twice-ear-approved line,
argued here, not inherited silently.

**The invite (L2).** A's "Take a guess before I show you" vs
the owner's own example phrase from his #54 feedback ("Can you
guess where it used to live?"). The owner's phrase is a
recorded preference from the finding this piece exists to
answer; A's version is not better, only different. **The
owner's phrase settles**, flagged delivery-role.

**The answer (L3).** A's "A living body was the archive"
plants the title word early — attractive, and rejected: the
piece's one deliberate jargon push is "archive", and spending
it in line 3 (before the viewer has the plain picture) breaks
the push's own rule — plain words first, the push cashed where
it pays (the epitaph, where the title lands). B's "a body was
the storage" is the plain form, and "storage" → "archive" is
the piece's vocabulary arc (the viewer learns the word by
earning it). **B settles.**

**The declaration (L4), proofs (L5–L6), compression (L7),
scale (L8).** A's re-voicings were weighed line by line; on
every one, A's difference was paraphrase, not improvement —
and two of A's were worse (the "written in…" metaphor, above;
"L6's 'that road'" for "that route" — 'route' is the word the
scene performs, a drawn line on a map). The delivered lines
are also load-bearing in the record's Chekhov table (the coat
and the water route are planted to fire inside the fatal
rule's two "before" clauses, word for word). **B/delivered
settle** — with the convergence stated plainly in §5 below.

**The fatal rule + sting (L9–L10).** B's R5 split (the sting
as its own performance line) is a delivery act with a recorded
reason this consolidation adopts: glued to a 28-word line, the
piece's best sentence reads as a tail; set apart, it reads as
the verdict it is. **B settles.**

**The plain statement (L11) — RESTORED, against B.** The v5
development dropped i7's L9 ("So say it plainly…") somewhere
in the v4 surgery and never recorded the cut as a decision.
The lane's close law is explicit (format v2, standing):
**the message stated once in plain words inside the
narration** (N-09) is a standard part of every episode's
close, before the bookend. A draft that drops a
law-mandated line without a recorded reason is carrying a
defect, not making a choice. **i7's line settles, restored**
— the one place this consolidation overrules the
editor-passed development, with the reason on record. (Its
absence in v5 is also a small proof of why this rebuild
exists: the old pipeline's decisions could vanish between
documents. In this branch the line lands in the transcript,
the scene, and the word-decision layer together.)

**The epitaph (L12).** B/delivered verbatim — the bookend
with its twist (the copying was having children *and watching
and learning*: both channels inside the return). Unimprovable
by A. **Settles.**

**The carry (L13–L14) — NEW; neither candidate had it.** The
old bridge spoke the banned formula (i7) or a cut-down ask
with no Academy and no named next thing (v5). The ENDING
STANDARD (2026-10-26) is newer law than both. A's two-line
form settles, tightened: L13 names the next home *inside the
topic* — and its last two words fire the piece's recall
device: **"the first backup"** answers L10's "there was no
backup" (the sting's words return as the next chapter's
name). L14 names the chapter, then plugs Hadosh Academy in
the piece's own grammar — homes of information, followed to
"the newest home of all: one you build, and own, yourself"
(the Academy's teaching — the harness as the archive you own —
stated without importing Academy vocabulary the piece hasn't
earned: no "harness", no "agent"; a cold viewer hears a
promise, an Academy viewer hears the doctrine).

ending are new; the teaching text is the piece's own,
