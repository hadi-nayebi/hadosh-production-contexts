   B's draft take may serve only lines whose settled text is

## §7 — Owner text rule recorded: the "lives" ban (2026-10-26, ~13:24 ET)

The owner ruled, effective immediately and standing: **the
word "lives" (L-I-V-E-S) is banned in any text destined for
synthesis** (transcripts / narration). The synthesis voices
pronounce it as the plural of "life," not as the verb, which
mixes the meaning. Replacements are per context (sits / runs /
stays / is kept / exists) or the line is restructured. Written
records may still quote the word as evidence — the ban governs
narration-feeding text, not the record of it.

Applied here:

- **Settled text (S1–S8): zero occurrences.** No line needed a
  replacement; the check was run over the final text, not
  assumed.
- **Candidate A: zero occurrences.**
- **Candidate B: zero occurrences** (one reason its take could
  be considered for reuse at all, phase 6).
- **The rejected i3** contains the word once, in i3-L6 — quoted
  in §3 as evidence under the written-record exception. It is
  one more mark of that text's distance from the owner's
  current direction; the text was already rejected on content.

The rule is carried into the phase-7 randomness register so it
travels with the package, and it binds any future line written
for this piece.
