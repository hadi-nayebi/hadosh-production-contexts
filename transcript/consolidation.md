# SOA-01 — transcript consolidation (phase 3, 2026-10-26)

This document is the consolidation the owner's process requires:
candidates considered in the open, the reasoning that settles the
text, and the settled transcript (`transcript/transcript.md`).
Nothing here is settled by inheritance. Candidate A is the first
draft's delivered text; it is adopted only where the line-by-line
weighing below says it earns adoption.

## What the transcript must do (from phases 1–2)

- Serve the objective: show 1.2 billion weekly users renting AI
  access while owning none of the layer around it.
- Obey the decided architecture: dateline → number → habit → turn
  at L4 → mechanism → aphoristic payoff → silent bridge.
- Answer the decided question in two halves: how many (the fact),
  and who owns any of it (the turn + payoff).
- Speak to the decided audience — the renters themselves — in
  doctrine language a newcomer can hold; canon-checked (the
  harness is the layer around the model; instructions, memory and
  files live inside it — canon §1–2).
- Fit the lane's form: one dated, sourced fact; ≈34 s; the number
  is the hook (arrives at L2).

## Candidate C — the fact-fallback wording (ruled a contingency, not a candidate)

The first draft's script record carries a documented fallback: if

weekly users (OpenAI, Aug 2026)" and only the number card changes.
Ruled here: this is a *contingency of the fact*, not a transcript
candidate — the 1.2B figure stands corroborated in the record

fallback stays on file in the randomness register as the

number the record does not support replacing.

## The weighing

**Architecture.** Candidate B spends its first line on a question
the viewer cannot yet feel — "who owns the AI you used" means
little before the scale lands. The decided payoff schedule
(number first → habit → turn) exists precisely so the turn has
something to cut against; B collapses it, turning at L3 with the
habit beat deleted entirely. B also breaks the lane's contract:
a State of Adoption report opens with its dateline, and B's
closing dateline ("That's the state of adoption — October 2026")
reads as a sign-off, not a report. B is rejected on structure,
not on taste.

**Line by line (Candidate A).**

- **L1** — The lane's dateline; it frames everything after it as a
  report with a date, which is what makes L2 a fact instead of a
  claim. Kept.
- **L2** — The fact and the hook in one line; number spelled out
  ("One point two billion") so the voice and the on-screen numeral
  peak together. Kept.
- **L3** — The correction rhythm ("Not tried it once. Used it")
  is the piece's most distinctive beat: it converts a usage
  statistic into a habit, and its repetition-with-emphasis is the
  delivery structure's named centrepiece. B deletes it and loses
  the visceral step the turn needs. Kept.
- **L4** — The turn, worded as an invitation ("the part nobody
  counts") rather than an accusation; "rents it" lands the
  rent/own frame in two words. Kept.
- **L5** — The mechanism, and the longest line (25 spoken words).
  Canon check: the chat box as "the thinnest harness there is" is
  canon-exact — a chat box is a harness reduced to nearly nothing,
  and the three things it fails to keep (instructions, memory,
  files) are precisely harness-interior contents under canon §1–2.
  The length is a delivery risk, not a content defect: the editor
  pass (delivery.md) flags it and records why it is paced by the
  three on-screen chips rather than cut. Kept, flagged.
- **L6** — The payoff IS the message (phase 1): essence and close
  in one aphorism, then the return to the lane's name. Kept.

**Audience.** A speaks to the renter without lecturing: it counts
*them* (L3's "a billion people"), names *their* situation (L5's
"their instructions, their memory, their files"), and lets the
bridge card make the personal appeal. B's second person ("the AI
you used") is more direct but spends the intimacy before earning
it. A kept.

## Decision

**Candidate A is adopted as this branch's settled transcript** —
because the weighing above sustains every line against the
objective, the architecture and the audience decided in phases
1–2, and the only genuinely different restructure on the table
(B) fails on structure. The settled text equals the first draft's
delivered text **as a conclusion, not as a copy**: if any line had
failed the weighing, this consolidation would have rewritten it,
and the failure would be recorded here instead.

Two conditions ride with the adoption:

1. **Fact currency.** The 1.2B figure is a September 29, 2026
   report. The piece is a dated report by design; the fallback
   wording (Candidate C) is the recorded route if the figure is
   ever superseded before a re-render. Register item R-2.
2. **L5's length** is accepted with the editor pass's pacing
   disposition (delivery.md), not silently.
