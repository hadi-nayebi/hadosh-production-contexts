## §2 — The owner's direction, as the revision's reason

His words, in substance (2026-10-26, ~14:22 ET):

(a) "The chained summary is that essentially you continue
creating a summary of the last N combining with the previous
summary, like a typical concept of a chain summary… you can

decisions, key feature requests, key criticisms, and that
will become the more defined version of the prompt, which is
summarized into its all key aspects."

(b) "What it is essentially describing is: the prompt is the
accumulation of the directions that the user gives to the
agent during a session — an agentic system during a session

that the context is going and producing… these are accumulated
steering of the user's feedbacks to the context."

(c) "In a good agentic system you can build these chain
summaries and extract certain context information from them
based on the instruction you use for the summaries, and that
will become a mega prompt idea that people can include in
their agentic systems — and even compartmentalize it by the
sessions or the jobs, that can build over multiple sessions."

(d) "That could help at the end to relay back to our direction
video: if you want to understand how a prompt can be a
direction, watch this other video — you can even plug that at
the end."

Process note (his): iterate quickly, four or five rounds
toward the final; this piece establishes the iteration pattern
for the others. And: no shortcut assumptions — if something is
unclear, ask. (The one ambiguity found — spoken plug vs
silent card for (d), and the fate of the WD-04 referral — is
