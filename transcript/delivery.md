# EO-EP1 — delivery (phase 3): the pause map + intonation

Stage 2 of the transcript (doctrine §0b): settled content
(`transcript.md`) gets its performance. **Every silence in
this piece is a recorded decision** — where, how long, what
for, on what basis (doctrine §1). Sizes come from the
doctrine's classes (§2), never from feel. The build assembles
the gaps FROM THIS MAP; delivered silences are measured
against it at phase 7 (doctrine §7: >150 ms off is a build
defect). The flat gaps of the old builds (0.40–0.60 s,
unchosen — defect D5) are the named anti-pattern.

**Intra-line beats (F7 — engine-owned, set by punctuation;
recorded because punctuation is a pause decision):**
- L3, at the colon ("…directly: | a body was the storage") —
  target ~400 ms: the plain statement steps forward out of
  its own sentence.
- L5, at the dash ("That pattern is information — | how to
  stay alive") — target ~300 ms: the concept key gets a
  half-beat of isolation.
- L9, at both dashes — ~200–400 ms each: the nested "before"
  clauses must stay parseable at first hearing.
- L12, at the dash ("It was a living body — | and the
  copying was…") — target ~300 ms before the closing list.
- L13, at the colon ("…could erase: | a story, told out
  loud") — target ~300 ms: the next home steps forward.
- L14, at the colon ("…home of all: | one you build") —
  target ~300 ms before the promise list lands.

## Intonation intent — per line

*The clone will not invent register; what is not marked here
is read flat. These intents bind the synthesis and any human
re-read; the punctuation + this map are the levers.*

|---|---|
| L1 | Neutral statement, then a REAL question — genuine rise on "live before that?", curiosity, not rhetoric |
| L2 | Light, inviting — a small smile in the voice; rise at the end (an invitation, not a quiz) |

| L4 | Signpost level, even; slight lift on "two ways" — the countable promise |
