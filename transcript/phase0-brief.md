# SOA-03 — Phase 0 Study Brief (State of Adoption, flagship fast lane)

Status: **Phase 0 complete 2026-10-23** (Archer). Scripts only — no build,
no upload. Lane rule (from SOA-01): one dated, sourced fact per short, read
against the Academy's prescription (own your harness).

## The fact

**In Gallup's Q3 2025 Workforce survey, 45% of U.S. employees said they
use AI at work at least a few times a year** — up from 40% in Q2 2025 and
21% in Q2 2023. Frequent use (a few times a week or more) reached 23%;
**daily use was only 10%.**

- **Source:** Gallup, "AI Use at Work Rises," published **December 2025**.
  https://www.gallup.com/workplace/699689/ai-use-at-work-rises.aspx
- **Method (from the source):** nationally representative survey of
  **23,068** U.S. adults employed full- and part-time, conducted online
  via the Gallup Panel, **August 5–19, 2025**, weighted to national
  demographics.
- **Corroboration:** eMarketer's summary of the same December 2025 Gallup
  report (45% at least a few times a year; more than double 2023's 21%):
  https://www.emarketer.com/content/nearly-half-of-workers-have-tried-ai-on-job

## Honesty flags (recorded, not hidden)

1. **"At least a few times a year" is a low bar — and the script says so.**
   The short's turn is built FROM the bar's lowness (only 10% daily); it
   never presents 45% as deep adoption.
2. **Industry spread is wide** (technology 76%, retail 33% in the same
   survey). The script speaks of "American employees" as Gallup reports
   them and does not claim uniformity.
3. **Pre-publish fallback:** if the 45% figure cannot be re-confirmed at
   build time, the Q2 2025 figure (40%) is from the same Gallup series
   and is named in the same article — swap the number and dateline only.

## The Academy read

Workplace adoption is broad but shallow: nearly half the workforce has
touched AI, one in ten uses it daily, and nearly all of it happens inside
tools the employer rents and configures. Nothing is set up around the
individual worker — no instructions of theirs, no memory of how they
work. The gap this short names: **adoption reached the whole office; a
personal practice reached almost nobody.**

- **Essence (≤8 words):** The office adopted; the worker rents.
- **Viewer restatement target:** "45% of U.S. employees have used AI at
  work, but only 10% use it daily — inside tools their employer rents."
