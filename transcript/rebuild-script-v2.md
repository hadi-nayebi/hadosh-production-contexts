## Editor Simulation #1 — on the rework draft

*Doctrine §6's six points, line-addressed.*

1. **Questions answered too fast (§3 audit).** The hook
   question at L1 ("Why does the machine treat cat and dog
   as almost the same word…?") is answered outright at L3 —
   the mechanism sentence IS the answer — with nothing
   between them but the orientation line and no suspension
   of any kind: no beat, no invite, no held frame in the
   narration. Question at 0:00, answer at 0:16.6, zero held
   breath. Second joint, the one the BADGE-GATE audit
   flagged (L7→L8): the piece's essence line — its answer,
   compressed — runs straight into the referral with no
   set-apart beat, so the exit spends the answer's landing.
2. **Transitions.** Phrases are honest — "So" at L6 does
   real conclusion work, L5 mirrors L4 by construction. But
   L6→L7 is glued: the naming and the essence return are two
   different speech acts (a verdict, then a summary) with
   no silence priced between them, and the fragment lists
   in L4/L5 have no recorded beats — as written they will be
   read as run-on tails of the quoted sentences.
3. **Intonation flattening.** Nothing is marked. L3 — the
   thesis — is the flattest-looking line in the piece and
   the one that can least afford it. L4/L5's quoted sentences
   need a register distinct from the narration around them
   or the "witnessed evidence" reads as more narration.
4. **The one thing (T-17), from the transcript alone:**
   "Words that share sentences end up pointing the same
   way — meaning-closeness is direction-closeness, from
   company, not spelling." Stated; taught. **PASS.**
5. **The pause map.** There isn't one. Eight lines, seven
   joints, seven intra-line performance beats — zero
   recorded pause decisions. Every silence in the build
   would be unchosen. The global objection.
6. **Navigation.** Recount: 13/128 = 10.2% — over the 10%
   target by one word, inside the hard cap. A content-layer
   property; recorded for the content owner, not a delivery
   defect this pass can repair.

**Editor's delivery score, rework draft: ≈ 38 / 100** — the
architecture is strong (which is content's credit), but
delivery as written is a sequence of unpriced joints, and
