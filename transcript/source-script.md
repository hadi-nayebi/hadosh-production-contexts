## ⚠ THE RULING, EMBODIED — PROVISIONAL (replaces Action #27)

i2 froze this script on a bare canon question. Per Hadi's
substantial-artifact rule (2026-10-24), the question is withdrawn
and the ruling now travels INSIDE the piece as a proposal he can
correct on the artifact:

**PROPOSED RULING — "prompt" is not a system part, and not a
rival term to Instructions.** A prompt is the text given *in the
moment* — one call's worth of input, part of the **active
context**. **Instructions** are the standing guidance stored in
the harness that steers *every* call. Prompt = typed once, lives
once. Instructions = set once, standing behind every chat.

- **Record basis — DERIVED from Hadi's own library** (exhaustion
  search 2026-10-24; full trail in the job file). The Academy
  writings use "prompt" consistently, and always as the moment's
  text against the standing structure:
  - *The Language of Agents* (vocabulary essay): "**Prompt
    engineering is choosing your words carefully.** Context
    engineering is designing the room so that a simple request
    is enough — the desk, the filing cabinets, **the rulebooks
    on the shelf**, the locks on the doors." The prompt is the
    chosen words / the simple request; the rulebooks are already
    on the shelf — standing.
  - *We Could Have Had AGI By Now* (B2): "**A prompt is
    consumed. A seed compounds.**" The prompt is used up in the
    call; what compounds is the durable structure.
  - *LLMs Are Not the Agents* (B1): "**prompt soup**" — "Put
    everything into **one enormous prompt** and you do not have
    a brain." The named anti-pattern is exactly cramming the
    standing layer into the one-off prompt.
  - Canon §3 ledger: *context* — "active context (the material
    in one model call — temporary) vs durable context (files,
    decisions, jobs, rules, memory that survive the call). The
    harness composes the first from the second"; *Instructions*:
    "Guidance that steers behavior through context."
  - **Nuance, recorded honestly:** the Job Lifecycle essay's
    *cumulative mega-prompt* — inside a job system, later
    prompts append into the job's interaction list, which the
    agent reads as its instruction set going forward. Prompts
    can be *kept* — but they become instruction-like only by
    being taken into the durable job record by the harness; the
    prompt itself is still consumed per call. L6 states the
    gloss; this nuance is the deep-dive layer and does not
    contradict it (canon §3's gloss rule).
- **Where it lives in the piece:** new beat **L6** states it in
  plain words (see script). The definition (L3/L8) is unchanged
  from i2 — the ruling changes the *framing*, exactly the part i2
  said could not be finalised without it.
