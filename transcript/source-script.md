# DIR-01 — "One Word, Two Directions" (Direction Series, Batch 1)

Status: **i1 build in production 2026-10-22** (Archer). Gate: Hadi
(Academy teaching). Series slate: files/direction-series-slate.md.

**Essence:** Context points the word.
**Viewer restatement:** "The word 'bank' has no fixed direction —
the words in front of it swing its arrow to the river's edge or to
the money house. That's how the machine knows which bank you mean."
**Title (designed):** One Word, Two Directions
**Cover (designed):** the L4 mid-swing frame — the arrow caught
between the two ghost headings — cover line: **WHICH BANK?**
**Funnel route:** Doorway (series entry).

## Script (locked for i1)

| # | Line | Intent |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | One word: bank. | The word alone; its arrow wobbles between two headings |
| L2 | By itself, it points nowhere. It could be the edge of a river — or the place your money sleeps. | The failure state: two ghost headings, no commitment |
| L3 | Now watch what context does. The river flooded the bank. | The experiment begins; the sentence arrives on the map |
| L4 | River got there first — and it pulls bank toward the water, toward the land's edge. Same word. New direction. | The swing, performed; payoff named |
| L5 | Reset it. I deposited my paycheck at the bank. | The controlled re-run, opposite pull |
| L6 | Paycheck pulls the other way — toward buildings, toward money. The word didn't change. Its direction did. | The mirror swing; the contrast lands |
| L7 | That's how the machine holds meaning: every word is a direction, and the words before it set where it points. | Return & name: the model in one sentence (essence restated) |

Form: semantic map on the WS01 axes (LAND↔WATER × BUILT↔LIVING) —
series continuity. Container v2, house style, captions per QC v2.0.
