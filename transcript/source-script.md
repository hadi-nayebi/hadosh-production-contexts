# SOA-04 — "84% Use It. 46% Don't Trust It." (State of Adoption, flagship fast lane)

Status: **Script draft i1, 2026-10-23** (Archer). Scripts only — no build,
no upload. Phase 0: `files/short-soa04-developers-phase0.md`. QC:
`files/qc-checklist.md` v2.12 (Section N gate below; anti-signature law
applied — see §6). Lane pattern: dateline → the fact → scale → the
ownership turn → close → bridge, in this piece's own words.

## 1. The fact (from Phase 0)

Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025, fielded **May 29 – June 23,
2025**, 49,009 responses: **84% of developers use or plan to use AI
tools** (44% in 2023); **46% actively distrust the accuracy of the
output, against 33% who trust it**.
https://stackoverflow.co/company/press/archive/stack-overflow-2025-developer-survey/

## 2. Narrative declaration (N-04 / N-06 — BEFORE drafting)

- **Structure: The Two Numbers** (lane structure, varied): adoption
  climbs (L2–L3), then the second number from the SAME survey turns
  the piece (L4) — the wow is that both numbers are true at once,
  about the same people.
- **Intended feeling:** respect, then recognition — "the fastest
  adopters are the least fooled; here's the work they do by hand
  that a layer should be doing."
- **Villain (N-06), named: The Hand-Check.** Consequence stack:
  (1) every output is re-verified manually, so the speed the tool
  promised leaks away in checking; (2) the checking knowledge lives
  in the developer's head and habits, not in a system — it cannot be
  reused, shared, or improved as an asset. Dead solution named by
  L5's shape: a better model is not the missing piece — the missing
  piece is the layer around whichever model (memory of the project,
  the user's own instructions, their own checks).
- **The ONE vivid detail (N-03):** "the number nobody puts on the
  poster" (L4) — the trust figure as the industry's unpublished
  statistic.
- **Jargon:** none pushed. "Layer" (L6) is defined by its three
  contents in L5 before it is named.

## 3. The one thing the viewer learns (T-17)

"Developers adopted AI fastest of anyone — 84% by 2025 — yet 46% of
them distrust its output, so they verify everything by hand: they
adopted the model, not the layer that would make it trustworthy."

Essence (≤8 words): **Builders adopted fastest, trust least.**

## 5. Retention reasoning — Decision Records (Q-0)

### DR-1 — THE CLICK (title + cover)
- **Choice:** Title: **"84% of Developers Use AI. 46% Don't Trust
  It."** Cover line: **"USED. NOT TRUSTED."** (3 words, S-09).
- **Alternatives considered:** title = the adoption number alone —
  rejected: adoption-only titles are commodity news; the collision
  of the two numbers is this piece's information gap. Cover "46%"
  alone — rejected: a bare number without its referent reads as a
  quiz, not a promise.
- **Context it serves:** the click promises a contradiction; the
  piece pays it with a single survey, which is also its credibility
  move — both numbers, one source.
- **What would change it:** if two-number titles measurably
  underperform on SOA-02/03 scoreboards, the trust number moves to
  the cover and the title keeps adoption only.

### DR-2 — THE CONTINUE (first sentence + re-earn map)
- **Choice:** L3 re-earns with velocity (44 → 84 in two years) and
  identity (the builders led); L4 is the hold — a promised hidden
  number, delivered inside the same line so the tease never becomes
  debt; L5 converts distrust into labour (the hand-check), which is
  what makes L6's "layer" land as a solution-shaped absence rather
  than a slogan.
- **Alternatives considered:** opening on distrust (46% first) —
  rejected: distrust without the adoption scale reads as "AI is
  failing," the opposite of the lane's report.
- **What would change it:** a dip at L4 on the scoreboard would mean
  the poster line reads as a tease too far; the fix is moving
  "forty-six percent" into L3's tail, not cutting the beat.

## 8. Honest weak points

1. Stack Overflow's sample is self-selected (Phase 0 flag 1). The
   script uses the survey's own headline language; the caveat lives
   in the Phase 0 where a researcher can find it, not in the
   narration. If Hadi wants sample-honesty in-piece (as the speech
   teaser does for its date), it costs one clause at L2 — flagged,
   not smuggled.
2. "What it writes" (L4) compresses the survey's "accuracy of AI
   tools' output." The antecedent is unambiguous in context, but a
   quote-mining comment could split the two; the description will
   carry the survey's exact item wording.
