# OBS-01-A — "The First Archive" (Observations · Episode 1, Short A)

Status: **Phase 1 script LOCKED 2026-10-21** (Archer). Workflow:

**Source:** "The Information System of a Planet," Observation 1 — beat (a):
information surviving in bodies. Archer-written wording; the claim is the
episode's.
**Essence:** The first archive was a body.
**Viewer restatement:** "Before writing existed, information survived by
being copied from one living body to the next — if the body died first,
the information died with it."
**Runtime target:** ~35 s (6 lines, 79 words).

## Script (locked)

| # | Line | Intent |
