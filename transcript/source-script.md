## The one thing the viewer learns (T-17)
"Before writing, information lived in living bodies, in two ways:
in the body itself — patterns like a spotted coat, passed to the
young — and in behavior — what the young learned by watching.
If it was not passed on, it was lost."

Essence (≤8 words): **Life stored information in bodies first.**

## Script (11 lines, 162 words — LOCKED)

1. Writing is only five thousand years old. So where did information
   live before that?
2. Inside living bodies. And I mean that directly: a body was the
   storage.
3. Bodies carried information two ways. In the body itself — and in
   behavior.
4. First, in the body. A coat of spots hides an animal from a lion.
   That pattern is information — how to stay alive. When the animal
   had young, the pattern was copied into a new body.
5. Second, in behavior. A calf learns the way to the water by
   following its mother. No gene carries that route. It is copied by
   watching, and by walking it, again and again.
6. Two kinds of information. One copied by birth. One copied by
   watching.
7. Copy after copy, both survived for millions of years.
8. But there was one fatal rule: die before you pass it on — before
   you have young, before the young have watched you — and your
   information dies with you. There was no backup.
9. So say it plainly: before there were books, information lived in
   bodies — and it could die with them.
10. The first archive on Earth was not paper or stone. It was a living
    body — and the copying was having children, and watching, and
    learning.
11. [Bridge, after the close — T-16] This is only the first chapter of
    how information evolved on Earth. Subscribe, like, and follow
    Earth One — the rest of the story is coming.

### N-06 Concrete villain
**Death with no backup.** Consequence stack: (1) a body dies
uncopied → its coat pattern ends AND its learned knowledge (where
to drink, when to run) ends; (2) the next generation starts
poorer, with no way to recover what was lost. No dead solutions
exist inside this world yet — nothing had been tried; writing is
the later solution and belongs to the series arc. Stated
explicitly per N-06's no-antagonist clause. PASS.
