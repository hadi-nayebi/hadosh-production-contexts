## 2. Narrative declaration (N-04 / N-06 — BEFORE drafting)

- **Structure: The House fills up** (lane structure, varied): one
  app's count (L2), its velocity (L3), then the walls around every
  one of those users (L4), then the house named (L5–L6).
- **Intended feeling:** awe at the arrival rate, then the quiet
  reframe — a crowd that size, and every one of them a guest.
- **Villain (N-06), named: The Landlord's Memory.** Consequence
  stack: (1) what the model remembers about you is kept inside
  Google's walls, on Google's terms — it serves the house, not the
  guest; (2) a guest who leaves takes nothing with them: the
  familiarity they built is not portable. Dead solution named by
  L4's shape: choosing the *other* landlord (SOA-01's box) changes
  the wallpaper, not the tenancy.
- **The ONE vivid detail (N-03):** a quarter of a billion people
  arriving in under half a year (L3) — a migration, not a growth
  curve.
- **Jargon:** none. "Guests / house" is the piece's own metaphor,
  introduced after the fact and cashed at the close (T-17d: the
  mechanism is stated plainly at L4 before the metaphor closes it).

## 5. Retention reasoning — Decision Records (Q-0)

### DR-1 — THE CLICK (title + cover)
- **Choice:** Title: **"Gemini Just Passed 650 Million Users."**
  Cover line: **"650 MILLION GUESTS."** (3 words, S-09) over the
  house cross-section visual (below).
- **Alternatives considered:** "Google's Second Front Door" —
  rejected: it presumes the viewer knows the first door (SOA-01) —
  a T-16 violation in packaging for a cold viewer. "A Quarter
  Billion Arrived" — rejected as the title: the velocity is the
  continue, not the click; the level (650M) is the tap.
- **Context it serves:** title states the level, cover states the
  reframe — the tap is promised the count AND the turn.
- **What would change it:** the lane's 48-h scoreboards; if
  reframe-covers beat level-covers twice running, SOA titles adopt
  the reframe form.

### DR-2 — THE CONTINUE (first sentence + re-earn map)
- **Choice:** L3 re-earns immediately with velocity (400M → 650M in
  five months — the number is still moving while you watch); L4
  slows the piece down on purpose — three "Google's" in one line
  make the wall felt — and L5's room image is the hinge the close
  cashes.
- **Alternatives considered:** using the newest chain point (1B,
  Aug 2026) as the fact — rejected in Phase 0 (thinner
  corroboration; the lane reports what it can source twice).
  Logged there, not hidden.
- **What would change it:** if a second independent source for the
  1B figure appears, a future SOA piece takes it as its own fact —
  this piece's dated 650M stands.

   description, as with SOA-01's Altman figure.

   aloud it must land as a drumbeat, not a stumble (T-08). If the
   TTS pass trips on it, the fallback is "inside Google's walls,
   under its rules, in its memory — not theirs," logged here in
   advance so the change is a decision, not a patch.
