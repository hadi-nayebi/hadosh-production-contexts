## 5. Retention reasoning — Decision Records (Q-0) [NEW in i2]

### DR-1 — THE CLICK (title + cover)
- **Choice:** Title: **"What Is Context?"** Cover: the desk from
  above, papers stacked, one mid-slide off the edge; cover line:
  **"IT FORGOT. IT'S NOT RUDE."** (5 words, S-09).
- **Alternatives considered:** cover line "ONLY WHAT IT CAN SEE"
  — rejected: it states the definition; the click should carry
  the viewer's own grievance (the forgot/rude pair is the
  complaint in their words). Title "Why Your AI Forgets You" —
  rejected: a why-title promises a troubleshooting video; the
  lane sells the word.
- **Context it serves:** this piece's tested variation is the
  empathy-reframe hook (declared in i1); the cover extends the
  reframe to the packaging so hook and packaging are one bet.
- **What would change it:** if reframe packaging underperforms
  the lane's contrast/hunt covers, the desk stays and the line
  becomes the definition form.

### DR-2 — THE CONTINUE (first sentence + re-earn map)
- **Choice:** L1 voices the viewer's sentence back at them and
  acquits the model in the same breath — the gap (if not rude,
  then what?) is the engine. L2 answers with a *sight* claim, L4
  gives it a place (the desk), L5 performs the forgetting as one
  visible motion — the piece's most replayable beat — and the
  split L6/L7 now gives the fix two landings instead of one
  crowded line, so the close (L8) arrives as a verdict, not a
  summary.
- **Alternatives considered:** keeping i1's single L6 — rejected
  in this round (change log item 1): three jobs in 20 words was
  the draft's own flagged defect.
- **What would change it:** a dip at L5 on the scoreboard would
  mean the slide needs to arrive earlier (swap L4/L5 staging),
  not that the desk is wrong.
