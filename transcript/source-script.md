**Essence:** A word is a direction.
**Viewer restatement:** "A computer turns a word into a list of numbers.
One number only ranks words; two numbers give each word an arrow from the
origin, and words that point the same way are related; real models use
thousands of numbers, so a word is a direction in a high-dimensional
semantic space — and any text, even an instruction, has a direction too."
**Runtime target:** ~90 s (10 lines, ~255 words).
