## Script v3 (11 lines — L6 changed, all else v2 verbatim)

1. A hand presses flat against a cave wall, and red dust is blown over it — forty thousand years ago.
2. The hand lifts away. The shape stays. It is still there today.
3. Until now, every message needed a living carrier — a body, a memory, a voice.
4. And carriers die. When the last person who remembers dies, the message dies too.
5. This hand needs nobody. It waits in the dark for whoever comes.
6. **And they came — children, hunters, strangers the maker never met — and to each of them the hand still speaks: I was here.**
7. This is one of the first messages that outlived its maker.
8. It cannot travel. Whoever wants it must come to the cave.
9. But it cannot be forgotten either. Stone does not forget.
10. The first archive was a body. The second, a story. The third is a mark on a wall, still speaking.
11. [Bridge, after the close — T-16] Earth One tells how information grew up on this planet — subscribe for the next chapter.

## DR-E3G-L6 — the one transcript change

- **Was (v2):** "And they came: children, hunters, strangers the
  maker never met — and the hand speaks to each of them: I was here."
  (24 words; the runtime's longest span, 8.13 s.)
- **Now (v3):** "And they came — children, hunters, strangers the
  maker never met — and to each of them the hand still speaks: I was
  here." (22 words.)
- **Why:** (a) Two colons in one breath — the list and the quote both
  hung off colons at the piece's emotional peak; the line was the
  hardest to say and to follow. The dash structure breathes:
  setup → arrival → speech. (b) "still" binds the episode's spine
  into a three-beat motif: L2 "still there today" → L6 "still
  speaks" → L10 "still speaking" — the word the whole piece is
  about now does structural work. (c) The accent set {speaks, here.}
  is untouched.
- **Alternatives:** leaving L6 (rejected — the colon pile-up is a
  real delivery friction at the peak); deeper cuts to L6 (rejected —
  the script's own weak-point note names L6 as the designated cut
  ONLY if measured timing breaks 75 s; the v3 build measures ~62 s).
- **Ceiling note (honest):** under surgery rules, the transcript's
  remaining distance from 100 is the guide-voice TTS performance,
  not wording. The blocker is the voice clone, parked on Hadi's
  reference recording. The v4 build keeps the music-only twin
  architecture so the clone swap lifts delivery without re-frames.

## Word choreography — per-word animation DECISIONS (Q-0)

Doctrine (Hadi, 2026-10-24): word behaviour is decided per beat and
recorded; words tell the story, they do not appear and disappear.
Tags are implemented in the scene engine and baked into the plan
per word; the table below is the decision of record (mirrored
in-scene as DR-E3G-CHOR).

| Line | Word | Behaviour | Story reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | dust | scatter — drifts up, thins out | the blown pigment disperses as it lands |
| 1 | forty | grow to 130% | deep time lands at full weight (the date label arrives with it) |
| 2 | stays. | hold — full size, unmoving | the shape stays; so does the word |
| 2 | still | grow + hold | the spine word, biggest of the line |
| 2 | today. | grow | the present arrives — with the real photograph |
| 3 | body, / memory, / voice. | settle — enter at 112%, settle to 85% | the carriers are handed on, each a little more fragile |
| 4 | die. | sink — falls, shrinks, dims | the carrier drops |
| 4 | remembers | fade to 45% | memory itself dims as it is spoken |
| 4 | dies, / too. | sink | the message falls with the carrier; the line ends low (and the picture cuts to embers) |
| 5 | nobody. | grow to 130% | the claim at full size: this hand needs NOBODY |
| 5 | waits | hold | waiting performed — the word simply stays |
| 6 | speaks | flip — turns to face the viewer | the mark turns toward the strangers — and toward us |
| 6 | here. | grow + hold | I WAS HERE — the largest word-scale of the piece |
| 7 | first | grow | the statement's stakes |
| 7 | outlived | hold | outliving = enduring on screen |
| 8 | travel. | tether — strains right, pulled back | it cannot travel; the word tries and cannot |
| 9 | forgotten | hold | refuses to fade |
| 9 | forget. | grow | stone's answer at full size |
| 10 | mark | grow | the third archive arrives larger than its elders |
| 10 | speaking. | flip + grow | the mark turns into a voice for the close |
| 11 | subscribe | grow | the ask is unmissable |

Every other word: the house entry (scale-in with the spoken moment,
key words amber with the overshoot pop) — itself a recorded default
