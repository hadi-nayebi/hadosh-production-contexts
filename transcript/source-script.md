## 2. The new beat — placement decision (Q-0 Decision Record)

- **Choice:** the Academy beat goes LAST (L12–L13), after the
  proof section (L8–L11) and before the cover close.
- **Alternatives considered:**
  - *After L1 (identity up front):* the viewer learns whose
    channel this is before learning what it does. Rejected: L2's
    promise ("how to use agentic AI as a tool") is the continue
    engine; inserting an organisation chart between the welcome
    and the promise spends the first five seconds on the least
    actionable fact in the piece.
  - *After L7 (before the proof):* "For now, let's build" flows
    into the Academy line, then L8's "This video is an example."
    Rejected: it splits the proof arc (example → open context →
    take it) from its setup and makes the Academy a premise the
    proof must carry, instead of a destination the proof earns.
  - *As a replacement for L11:* rejected outright — L11 is the
    piece's thesis ("we make the thing, and we share how it's
    made") and the approved v3 close of the teaching arc; the
    Evolution Plan adds a beat, it does not evict one.
- **Context it serves:** by L12 the viewer has seen the promise
  (L2), the reframe (L3–L4), the mechanism (L5), and the proof
  (L8–L11). The Academy is named at the exact moment the viewer
  can ask "who is behind this, and where do I start?" — L13
  answers both halves. The intro thereby gains the one thing v3
  lacked: an off-YouTube home for the funnel (the funnel ruling
  routes shorts to the long-forms; the intro now also routes to
  the Academy site).
- **Why this one:** the beat is an INVITATION, and invitations
  land after value is demonstrated. It also keeps v3's first 11
  lines byte-identical, so v4 inherits v3's locked narration
  (production note in §5).
- **What would change it:** if the 48-h scoreboard pattern on
  intro-as-trailer shows drop-off before L12, the beat moves to
  after L7 (the runner-up placement) rather than being cut — the
  Academy connection is the point of v4.

## 4. Retention reasoning (Q-0)

### DR-1 — THE CLICK
This piece is the channel trailer for non-subscribers; its
"click" is the channel-page visit itself and its cover close is
the tap surface. **Choice:** cover close unchanged from v3
("LET'S BUILD IT TOGETHER.") — the name the whole piece has just
glossed twice (L1, L12). **Alternative:** a promise-led cover
("BUILD YOUR OWN SOFTWARE") — rejected for v4: changing the
cover AND adding the beat in one iteration would make the two
changes unmeasurable against each other; the Evolution Plan's
rule is one beat at a time. **What would change it:** a dedicated
cover iteration (v5 candidate) once v4's beat has data.

### DR-2 — THE CONTINUE
- L1–L2 (unchanged): the promise in the first breath — one thing,
  learnable, a tool in your hands.
- L3–L4: the reframe is the hold — the viewer who came with the
  "Artificial" picture is told the channel means the other one.
- L8: the proof turn — "this video is an example" converts the
  intro from a claim into evidence; L9–L10 hand the evidence
  over ("take it").
- ★ L12–L13: the new final re-earn — after the hand-over, the
  piece answers the question the proof creates ("whose method
  is this, where does it live?") and gives the viewer a next
  step OFF the platform (the Academy site) plus the return loop
  ("come back and build here"). The intro now ends with a
  destination, not only a slogan.
- **Removal test (N-08 discipline):** delete L12–L13 and v4 is
  v3 — complete, but the funnel's top has no exit toward the
  Academy; that absence is exactly what the Evolution Plan
  exists to close.
