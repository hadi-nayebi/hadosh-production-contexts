## L1 — (M1, the flat picture)

Most people think a prompt is the first sentence you type into the chat.

## L2 — (M2, the turn)

It is not. A prompt is a direction — it points the context somewhere.

## L3 — (M3, the steering performed — the first direction)

Say: build me a page. That is one direction — the work starts moving.

## L4 — (M3, the steering performed — steer again)

Then you steer again: warmer. Shorter. For my mother, not for engineers.

## L5 — (M4, the context underneath)

Meanwhile the agent keeps producing the context — continuing it, compacting it — and every prompt you add bends it a new way.

## L6 — (M5, accumulation)

So a prompt does not happen once, at the start. It accumulates — direction after direction, across the whole job.

## L7 — (M6, the gathering)

Take every prompt from a session, chain them into one summary, and you hold the mega prompt.

## L8 — (M6, the payoff)

That is what a prompt really is: not one sentence — all the directions you gave to re-steer the work.
