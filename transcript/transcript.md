1. Writing is only five and a half thousand years old. So
   where did information live before that?

   body.
6. Second, in behavior. A calf learns the way to the water by
   following its mother. No gene carries that route. It is
   copied by watching, and by walking it, again and again.
7. Two kinds of information. One copied by birth. One copied
   by watching.
8. Copy after copy, both ways carried it for millions of
   years.
9. But there was one fatal rule: die before you pass it on —

10. There was no backup.
11. So say it plainly: before there were books, information
    lived in bodies — and it could die with them.
12. The first archive on Earth was not paper or stone. It was

    watching, and learning.
13. But information found a home no single death could erase:
    a story, told out loud — the first backup.
14. That is the next chapter on Earth One. And Hadosh Academy
