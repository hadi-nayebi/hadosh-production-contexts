Lane: `hidden_files/study-jobs/` · Cron: `study-effectiveness-review` (pointer body; steps live in INSTRUCTION.md)

## What this job is
The lane's own review duty. The owner names the failure mode directly: production that is "a verification ceremony and showcase without real substance." This job exists to catch exactly that — in this lane. Its sole question is **utility: is what the lane creates actually usable, and actually used, by future jobs?** It produces nothing for the library; it judges the library, the packets, the recreations, and the lane's own reporting, and its verdicts go to HQ like the lane's other reports.

## Success, stated so a cold reader can test it
Each weekly cycle files verdicts — one per asset class (technique catalogue · markdown assets · recreations · packets as reading layer · growth reporting itself) — each verdict one of **USED** (consumption evidence cited) · **USABLE-UNUSED** (a cold pull test passes but no consumption yet; the clock is named) · **CEREMONY** (produced, counted, and not usable or not used — with the priced cause: wrong topics? bad indexing? no demand? unusable form?). Every verdict cites its evidence: consumption log lines, a timed cold-pull test run by this job, tier/score records. A **CEREMONY verdict is a headline** — it leads the report to HQ, never a footnote. A cycle that finds everything USED must show the consumption evidence for each class or the verdict does not stand.

## Scope — in / out
- IN: reading the lane's faces (INDEX · TECHNIQUES · ASSETS · GROWTH · CONSUMPTION), running cold-pull tests, pricing unconsumed classes, filing verdicts + the HQ headline.
- OUT: fixing what it finds (cures route to the owning job via the lane board — this job judges, it does not quietly repair its own findings away); producing assets; scoring recreations (recreation job + Codex own that).

## Shape decisions
- Cadence: weekly at minimum (cron 168h). The first cycle runs when the funnel has a week of record OR the first production pull lands, whichever comes first — an earlier founding record states the baseline (day one: consumption 0, everything USABLE-UNUSED at best, clock started 2026-11-13).
- Form: TLDR-form — compartmentalized, judgment-first, verdicts before counts, in the fleet's TLDR discipline.
- This job is itself subject to its own question: if two consecutive cycles produce verdicts nothing acts on, the job files that as a finding about itself.
