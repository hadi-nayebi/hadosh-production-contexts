## 1. What it judges
- Deliverable: a draft effectiveness verdict report (per asset class: USED / USABLE-UNUSED / CEREMONY, with the clock accounting).
- Call point: effectiveness-review INSTRUCTION §5 Verify, at the §6 filing. The verdict is advisory input to the filing decision: applied, a REFUSE keeps the draft unfiled and the lane hears "no verdict this cycle" + the reason, never a softened verdict; set aside, the miss is recorded in Condense. An unfired call makes the cycle INCOMPLETE.

## 2. Instruction (the model's charge)
Judge whether each class verdict is EARNED by the evidence cited for it. Price: (1) CONSUMPTION EVIDENCE — a USED rests on actual pull records (CONSUMPTION rows with production citations), not on availability, impressions, or the lane's hopes. (2) USABILITY EVIDENCE — a USABLE rests on demonstrated retrievability (naive-use/pass evidence), not on the asset merely existing. (3) CLOCK ACCOUNTING — is the elapsed-time basis for CEREMONY stated honestly (verdict clock start, pulls window), so a young lane is not condemned for being young NOR excused past its clock? (4) CLASS DISCIPLINE — is every registered class verdicted (no silent omissions), and is CEREMONY called where the evidence earns it — including when the uncomfortable class is the lane's favorite? Score 0–1 per dimension and classify defects. You judge the verdict's warrant, never the lane's effort.

## 3. Context contract (masked view)
- FED: the draft verdict report · CONSUMPTION.md (full) · GROWTH.md's current counts · the naive-use/pass evidence cited · the verdict-clock record.
- NEVER FED: the reviewer's rationale prose · HQ's hopes for the lane · prior cycles' verdicts on other classes (each class priced on its own evidence).
- Ceiling: ≤32k tokens.

## 4. Output space
- Verdict classes (on the draft): **FILE · FILE-WITH-CORRECTIONS · REFUSE**.
- Dimensions (0–1): consumption evidence · usability evidence · clock accounting · class discipline.
- Rejection taxonomy (one primary): UNEARNED-USED (USED without pull records) · UNEARNED-USABLE (no retrievability evidence) · CLOCK-FUDGE (elapsed basis misstated or missing) · MISSING-CLASS · SOFTENED-CEREMONY (evidence earns CEREMONY; draft prints milder).
- Accepted profiles travel: the profile files with the verdict report; HQ sees the warrant strength beside the verdicts.
