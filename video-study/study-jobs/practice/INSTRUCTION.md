## §0 — Cycle shape (WAVE form — throughput correction, HQ 2026-11-14)
- Trigger: cron `study-practice` or HQ charge. **A cycle is a WAVE, not an item:** up to **4 pool items** per cycle (starting price), filled in pick order (lowest average first, stalest breaks ties), each re-attempted once and re-scored.

- **Batch re-pricing:** measured per-item cost from the first waves re-prices the wave size at Condense, reported to HQ — 4 is a starting price.
