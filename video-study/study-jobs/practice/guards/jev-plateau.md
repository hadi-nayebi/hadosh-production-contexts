# Jev call — jev-plateau (practice)

_Per-call definition from jev-call-template.md. Designed 2026-11-14. The judgment: the floor rule (Amendment A2) turns on whether a non-beating attempt's WHY names a real plateau — this call is that judgment's owner, so "plateau" can never become a rubber stamp._

## 1. What it judges
- Deliverable: a non-beating re-attempt's WHY filing in the artifact's scores.md (cause · lens attribution · next move).
- Call point: practice INSTRUCTION §5.4 — this call fires at its step on every cycle; its verdict is advisory input to the cycle's close (applied, or set aside with the miss recorded in Condense). A cycle in which it did not fire prints INCOMPLETE.

## 2. Instruction (the model's charge)
Judge whether this WHY explains a plateau or excuses one. Price: (1) CAUSAL SPECIFICITY — does it name the mechanism that capped the score (a construction limit, a priced trade-off, a defect interaction), in terms a builder could act on? (2) LENS ATTRIBUTION — is the cap tied to the filed lens returns that measured it, or asserted? (3) CONSEQUENCE — does it state what changes next (a different construction, a named escalation to HQ, retirement of the approach) — a WHY whose next move is "try again harder" has no consequence? (4) EVIDENCE CITATION — does it point at the attempt's own records (build record, returns, measurements)? Score 0–1 per dimension and classify. A plateau is a property of the APPROACH, demonstrated; difficulty is not a plateau, and effort is not evidence.

## 3. Context contract (masked view)
- FED: the WHY filing · the two attempts' filed lens scores + the returns the WHY cites · the item's build records (cure lists attempted).
- NEVER FED: the builder's effort narrative · elapsed-time claims (a plateau is not measured in hours).
- Ceiling: ≤32k tokens.

## 4. Output space
- Verdict classes: **PLATEAU-NAMED · PLATEAU-UNNAMED**. **Amendment A-C004-3 (2026-11-14, Cycle-004, CANDIDATE — untested as a fired class; until this call's first firing, the seat's own judging stands in, printed as such): a third class, WHY-ACCEPTED-NO-PLATEAU — a non-beating WHY that meets every dimension floor below (≥0.5, consequence >0) and whose classification is a named regression/interaction mechanism with a priced next discriminating test satisfies the floor rule WITHOUT claiming a plateau.** (Evidence: Cycle-004 T16 attempt 3 — the floor rule's first non-beating WHY names a regression mechanism (trajectory integration → registration drift), explicitly declines a plateau claim, and prices attempt 4's discriminating test; the binary had no lawful home for that filing.)
- Dimensions (0–1): causal specificity · lens attribution · consequence · evidence citation.
- Rejection taxonomy (one primary): VAGUE-CAUSE · UNATTRIBUTED (no lens evidence) · NO-CONSEQUENCE · EFFORT-AS-EVIDENCE.
- Accepted profiles travel: the verdict + profile append to scores.md beside the WHY; a NAMED plateau escalates to HQ as a headline (per Amendment A2) carrying this profile.

## 5. Floors and disposition
- Any dimension < 0.5 → PLATEAU-UNNAMED · consequence = 0 → UNNAMED at any other score.
- An UNNAMED verdict = the lane deviation PLATEAU-UNNAMED: the cycle does not close; the WHY is rewritten against the classification and re-judged once; a second UNNAMED escalates to HQ as a stall, not a filing.
