## 1a. Voluntary class-level filings (amendment landed 2026-11-19, Cycle-031 Condense — closing the cross-check carried since Cycle-029)

This call's treatment of such a filing: (a) the mandatory trigger above (§1 — a non-beating attempt's WHY) is unchanged and is NOT discharged by any class filing; (b) a voluntary class filing is judged on the same four dimensions (causal specificity · lens attribution · consequence · evidence citation) applied to the class's own record (its pre-committed legs, the primary lens's returns, the cure stage's evidence); (c) its verdict classes are the class's own — the test FIRED with its disposition (rest as constructed / family change) or NOT FIRED with its legs printed — and a fired class test is never re-labelled a plateau verdict on the attempt; where a seat-named plateau carries a prescribed construction, A-C030-1's two-instrument printing governs (the class test and the escalation's cure test print separately, neither discharging the other); (d) a voluntary filing judged PLATEAU-UNNAMED on its class record is a filing defect of that entry, cured in the same close, and does not hold the cycle open the way an UNNAMED mandatory WHY does (§5). Forcing evidence: Cycles 029–031 exercised both forms repeatedly (T16 a9/a10 · T17 a11 · T11 a6 · T02 a3 voluntary filings beside T23 a16's mandatory WHY) while this definition's text covered only the mandatory path (Codex Condense finding 4, Cycle-031, closing the charge on its third Condense).

## 2. Instruction (the model's charge)
Judge whether this WHY explains a plateau or excuses one. Price: (1) CAUSAL SPECIFICITY — does it name the mechanism that capped the score (a construction limit, a priced trade-off, a defect interaction), in terms a builder could act on? (2) LENS ATTRIBUTION — is the cap tied to the filed lens returns that measured it, or asserted? (3) CONSEQUENCE — does it state what changes next (a different construction, a named escalation to HQ, retirement of the approach) — a WHY whose next move is "try again harder" has no consequence? (4) EVIDENCE CITATION — does it point at the attempt's own records (build record, returns, measurements)? Score 0–1 per dimension and classify. A plateau is a property of the APPROACH, demonstrated; difficulty is not a plateau, and effort is not evidence.
**Voluntary class filings (§1a path):** the same charge applies to a class filing's own record — price the class's mechanism (not the attempt's average), its attribution to the class's primary-lens returns, the consequence its disposition carries (rest / family change / escalation cure test), and its citation of the cure stage's evidence. The judge returns the class's own verdict (test FIRED with disposition, or NOT FIRED with legs), never a plateau verdict on the attempt.

## 4. Output space
- Verdict classes: **PLATEAU-NAMED · PLATEAU-UNNAMED**. **Amendment A-C004-3 (2026-11-14, Cycle-004, CANDIDATE — untested as a fired class; until this call's first firing, the seat's own judging stands in, printed as such): a third class, WHY-ACCEPTED-NO-PLATEAU — a non-beating WHY that meets every dimension floor below (≥0.5, consequence >0) and whose classification is a named regression/interaction mechanism with a priced next discriminating test satisfies the floor rule WITHOUT claiming a plateau.** (Evidence: Cycle-004 T16 attempt 3 — the floor rule's first non-beating WHY names a regression mechanism (trajectory integration → registration drift), explicitly declines a plateau claim, and prices attempt 4's discriminating test; the binary had no lawful home for that filing.)
- Dimensions (0–1): causal specificity · lens attribution · consequence · evidence citation.
- Rejection taxonomy (one primary): VAGUE-CAUSE · UNATTRIBUTED (no lens evidence) · NO-CONSEQUENCE · EFFORT-AS-EVIDENCE.
- Accepted profiles travel: the verdict + profile append to scores.md beside the WHY; a NAMED plateau escalates to HQ as a headline (per Amendment A2) carrying this profile.
- Voluntary class output: the verdict (FIRED + disposition, or NOT FIRED + legs) + profile append to scores.md beside the CLASS FILING (not beside any WHY); a fired class disposition travels to the next Plan as a named charge; no HQ headline is owed for a voluntary firing (the headline duty binds mandatory NAMED plateaus).

## 5. Floors and disposition
- Any dimension < 0.5 → PLATEAU-UNNAMED · consequence = 0 → UNNAMED at any other score.
- An UNNAMED verdict = the lane deviation PLATEAU-UNNAMED: the cycle does not close; the WHY is rewritten against the classification and re-judged once; a second UNNAMED escalates to HQ as a stall, not a filing.
- Voluntary class disposition: the §5 hold above binds the mandatory WHY path only. A voluntary class filing judged defective on its class record is an entry defect: the filing is rewritten against the classification and re-judged once in the same close; it does not hold the cycle open, and its re-judgment prints beside the class filing.
