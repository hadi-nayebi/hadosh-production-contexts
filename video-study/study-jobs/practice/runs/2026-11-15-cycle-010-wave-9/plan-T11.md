### CONVERGENT 1 — [M+R] Fractional roll settle (attempt-3 WHY K2; motion 73 + readability 58)

*Finding (both returns, source-confirmed):* `rollPositions(c, value)` derives every column from the total value — `(value / place) % digitBase` — so at settle the hundreds column sits at strip position **2.6** for target 260 (likewise 3.8, 5.2; only 800 aligns), with partial adjacent digits visible in the clipped cells. The displayed *value* is integral; the displayed *strips* are not. Attempt 3's own-checks asserted the integer digit mapping (`digitsOf`) and roll continuity but never the displayed strip positions — an A-C008-2-class checks gap (suite written from the build's state vocabulary), named by A-C009-2 as this item's founding evidence.

*Construction change (C2 in the WHY list, taken first here by convergence):*
1. Replace the total-value-proportional roll with **per-column progression**: each column's strip position is driven by *its own digit's* progression — the ones column rolls continuously through its count; a higher column advances only as its own digit changes (stepping/easing to the next integer as the digit increments), so at span end every column's position is exactly its settled digit (integral by construction, not by rounding at the end).
2. Acceptable equivalent, if the re-observation in Execute step 1 favours it: keep a continuous roll but **ease the fractional remainder to the settled digit within the final fraction of the span** (the WHY's own alternative) — the invariant is identical: at settle, and at every settle checkpoint, each displayed strip position is an integer.
3. Preserve what attempt 3 got right and the motion lens credited: genuine rolling (not counting), monotonic approvals rise, no counter blur, exact integer settle of the value. The modulo backward jumps the motion return also named are priced under this cure: per-column progression must not introduce a backward jump at settle checkpoints (a column easing *forward* to its digit is lawful; a strip snapping backward is the priced defect recurring).
