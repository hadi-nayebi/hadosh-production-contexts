### C1 — TURN CHAINING → distinct turn per page change, window derived from the interval it lives in (CONVERGENT: motion 74 + timing 85 + readability 80)

**Defect (attempt-4 returns, verbatim substance):** `retargetPage()` restarts `turnT0` only after `turnMs=140` completes (`if (now - turnT0 >= CONFIG.turnMs) turnT0 = now;`), while rapid changes arrive every 87.5 ms — so multiple counter changes share one compression-and-return gesture; some pages change texture inside another page's turn, sitting compressed at travelling intensity 0.38 (below the stack's 0.60) for much of the rapid span. Timing: "budget visible turn completion alongside particle travel; verify page readability independently of `isSettled()`."

**Construction changes (all in `recreation.html`):**
1. **Restart per change:** `retargetPage()` sets `turnT0 = now` on EVERY page change (the non-restart guard is deleted). Each page change owns exactly one turn gesture.
2. **Span-derived turn duration (A-C006-4 construction note):** the effective turn duration for a change is derived from that change's own interval: `turnEff = min(CONFIG.turnMs, intervalMs − CONFIG.minSettledHoldMs)`, where `intervalMs` comes from `computeSchedule()`'s dwell table for that page. `turnMs` (140) remains the CONFIG cap and governs the early pages; the rapid pages derive theirs. The turn profile (`turnU`, compression factor) divides by `turnEff` for the active turn, not by the raw cap.
3. **Turn runs CONCURRENT with arrival, never serial after it:** the turn window opens at the page-change instant, the same instant travel/stagger open. Compression is applied to front-page anchors as today (toward the page left-edge pivot, `turnCosMin` floor), in parallel with the 28 ms travel + ≤8.4 ms stagger. A serial construction (turn after arrival) is arithmetically infeasible — priced in (e) below — and the budget assertion in (d) is written to catch it.
4. **Budget honesty:** `computeSchedule()`'s budget gains the turn term under the concurrent model: budget = max(arrival 36.4, turnEff) + reserved settled hold 20 ≤ interval. `enforceBudget()`'s refusal path extends to this budget: if a swapped `pageCount`/`flipMs` makes even the derived turn unable to leave the reserved hold, the combination is refused (reset to DEFAULTS + warning), exactly as the travel budget already refuses.
5. **Readable-state predicate:** a new `isReadable(p, now)` = borrowed ∧ captured ∧ travel elapsed ∧ **turn complete for the current page** ∧ **registration complete** (see C7). The runtime settlement suite (d) asserts against `isReadable`, never against `isSettled` alone — the attempt-4 timing catch (settlement predicate excluding turn + registration) may not recur by construction.

**Interval arithmetic (priced against the preserved anchors):**

| Span | Interval | Arrival (travel 28 + stagger ≤8.4) | turnEff = min(140, interval − 20) | Turn vs arrival | Guaranteed uncompressed, settled tail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Early (pages 1–5) | 300 ms | ≤36.4 ms | 140 ms (cap binds) | turn outlasts arrival by 103.6 ms | 300 − 140 = **160 ms** |
| Rapid (pages 6–13) | 87.5 ms | ≤36.4 ms | **67.5 ms** | turn outlasts arrival by 31.1 ms | 87.5 − 67.5 = **20 ms** (= `minSettledHoldMs`, by construction) |

- Serial alternative, priced and rejected: 28 + 8.4 + 67.5 + 20 = **123.9 ms > 87.5 ms** — infeasible inside the rapid interval; concurrency is a construction requirement, not a style choice.
- Turn visibility under the derived window: 67.5 ms = ~4 frames at 60 fps (16.667 ms), ≥1 frame at 50 ms steps guaranteed only if a step lands inside the window — the (d) visibility assertion samples 4 phase offsets × both cadences and requires ≥1 frame with mean front compression ≤0.90 for **every** rapid change at 60 fps and ≥80% at 50 ms steps (attempt-4's T-TURN thresholds, now per-change instead of chained).
