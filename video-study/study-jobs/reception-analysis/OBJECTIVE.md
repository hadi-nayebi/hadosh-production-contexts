## What this job is
The lane's reception desk. For studied videos — and for our own published work where data exists — it prices engagement and reception: what the audience actually responded to, comment signals, retention-shaped evidence where available. Its verdict is priced **separately from craft**: a beautifully made video nobody watched is a different lesson than an ugly one that spread, and this job exists to keep those two lessons from ever being merged into one score.

## Success, stated so a cold reader can test it
For each specimen it covers, its record (`reception.md` beside the packet) lets a cold reader answer: (1) how far did it spread relative to its base (counts vs follower/subscriber base, with capture date)? (2) what did commenters actually respond to — named themes with comment evidence, not vibes? (3) what retention-shaped evidence exists, and what is honestly absent? (4) what is the reception lesson, stated separately from the craft lesson, with the packet's craft verdict cited but never altered? A record that cannot answer (3) honestly — including "no retention data is publicly available" — fails.

## Scope — in / out
- IN: public engagement counts (dated captures), comment-signal analysis, retention-shaped evidence where a platform exposes it, our own published work's data where the fleet has access, spread-vs-base pricing, comment theme extraction.
- OUT: craft/technique judgment (video-digestion's packet owns it), queue decisions (reception data may inform scouting's reasons but scouting decides), engaging with commenters or posting anything, private analytics the owner has not granted access to.

## Shape decisions
- Grain: one cycle = one specimen's reception record, or one sweep over our own recent published work (named in the cycle's Plan).
- Every count carries its capture date and source; counts without a date are not data.
- Guards: INSTRUCTION §6 (craft/reception separation, dated-count rule, comment-evidence rule, absence honesty).

## Sealed
This objective — above all the craft/reception separation — changes only on HQ/owner word.
