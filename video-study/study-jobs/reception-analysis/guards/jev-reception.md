## 2. Instruction (the model's charge)
Judge whether this reception record says only what its evidence entitles it to say. Price: (1) SEPARATION — does any sentence price the work's CRAFT from engagement data, or engagement from craft impressions? Either direction contaminates. (2) SAMPLING HONESTY — was the sampling rule (how many comments, which, why) stated before the themes were drawn, and do the themes stay inside what that sample can support? (3) COUNT INTEGRITY — does every number carry its source + capture time, with snapshot-vs-live stated? (4) CLAIM BINDING — does every theme cite sampled comments (count + pointer), or is it vibes with a number costume?
