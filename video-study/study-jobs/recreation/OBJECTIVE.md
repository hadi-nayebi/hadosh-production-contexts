## What this job is
The lane's proof layer. The owner's ruling: a PDF digest alone is not effective study — effective learning is RECREATION. For every technique the lane promotes to complete, this job rebuilds it in HTML — with HTML's own abilities (CSS/JS animation, generated imagery + overlays), never by re-embedding or tracing the video — composes the side-by-side comparison (original segment cut beside the live recreation, synchronized playback), submits the recreation to a Codex scoring wave, and files the scores into the catalogue entry. Only a passing recreation consolidates the technique as RECREATED; the PDF remains the reading layer, the HTML is the proof.

## Success, stated so a cold reader can test it
A technique counts as recreated when its directory under `hidden_files/study/recreations/` holds: (1) the original segment cut (evidence, clearly labeled as the original — never presented as the recreation); (2) `recreation.html` — a parameterized build passing the swap test (change the config's words/timing/palette and the technique still works); (3) `comparison.html` — the side-by-side face with synchronized playback; (4) `scores.md` — the Codex wave's per-lens scores + the consolidation verdict against the bar (average ≥85 AND no lens under 70). A production job opening the recreation in Observe can lift the HTML machinery itself into its own build. Anything less is IN PROGRESS with its scores and priced gap printed — never silently "done".

## Scope — in / out
- IN: segment cutting from archived sources; parameterized HTML recreation builds; comparison composition; Codex scoring waves; score filing into catalogue entries (via style-library hand-off); recreation queue upkeep in `../QUEUE.md`.
- OUT: digesting new specimens (video-digestion); tier bookkeeping in the library files themselves (style-library owns INDEX/TECHNIQUES — this job files the evidence + verdict, style-library moves the tier); production adoption of a technique into a shipping short (production's own laws govern that — a recreation proves a technique, it does not clear production's asset laws); altering a packet's content.

## Shape decisions
- Grain: one cycle = one WAVE of up to 5 owed techniques (throughput correction, 2026-11-14), fanned out in parallel, each through to a scored verdict (PASS consolidates, or FAIL filed with scores + priced gap). A technique needing a second attempt goes to the practice pool on the same directory — attempts accumulate, scores never overwrite.
- Queue: the recreation queue in `../QUEUE.md` — owner-named techniques first, then production-adjacent (word animation · transitions · openings), each with its reason. Not every described technique owes a recreation; the queue decides, in the open.
- Tiers (enforced by style-library): DESCRIBED (packet evidence only) · IN PROGRESS (attempted, below bar) · RECREATED (passing HTML on file). Only RECREATED is complete.
- Guards: INSTRUCTION §6 (construction purity, swap test, scoring integrity, no-captions, segment honesty).
