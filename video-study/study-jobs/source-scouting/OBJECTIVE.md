# source-scouting — OBJECTIVE

`hadosh-job-file:v1` · born 2026-11-13 (HQ founding charge, video study lane) · standing job, bounded OPEVC cycles
Lane: `hidden_files/study-jobs/` · Cron: `study-source-scouting` (pointer body; steps live in INSTRUCTION.md)

## What this job is
The lane's intake. It continuously finds candidate videos worth studying — creators and formats, trending motion-graphics work, references the owner sends, and styles adjacent to what production is building next — and enters each into the lane queue (`../QUEUE.md`) with a one-line reason stating what production would learn from it. Owner sends enter the queue at the top, above everything scouted.

## Success, stated so a cold reader can test it
The queue never silently empties: at any moment, `../QUEUE.md` shows either (a) ≥3 live candidates, each with specimen identity (creator, platform, URL), a one-line learn-reason naming the production surface it feeds, and its entry source; or (b) a dated empty-queue finding in this job's latest run record explaining the drought and what was searched. A candidate whose reason could apply to any video ("good editing") has not been scouted — it has been listed.

## Scope — in / out
- IN: candidate discovery (web/social search, owner-send intake, creator tracking), queue entry + ordering, backlog promotion/retirement with reasons, adjacency mapping to production's next builds.
- OUT: digesting (video-digestion), reception verdicts (reception-analysis — a candidate's view counts are intake data, not a reception finding), contacting creators, any outward action in the owner's name.

## Shape decisions
- Grain: one cycle = one scouting sweep producing 0–5 queue entries + a queue state report. Quality of reasons over count.
- Sources, in priority order: (1) owner sends (top of queue, always); (2) styles adjacent to production's current/next containers (read production's record first — DLC lane + studio notes); (3) creators already in the owner's study record (`hidden_files/study/notes/` names several: cinematic 3D journey, quiet diorama 3D, kinetic typography); (4) trending motion-graphics work found by search.
- Guards: INSTRUCTION §6 (reason-specificity, identity-completeness, owner-send priority, no-duplication against the library index).

## Sealed
This objective changes only on HQ/owner word. Queue ordering rules (owner sends on top) are sealed likewise.
