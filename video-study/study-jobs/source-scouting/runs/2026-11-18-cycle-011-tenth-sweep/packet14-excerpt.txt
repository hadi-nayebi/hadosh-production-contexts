## 3. Technique catalogue

### T01 — Line-icon figure system with a star-marked protagonist
- **Timestamps:** throughout; protagonist board 0–13 s, solo figure 142–155 s.
- **Mechanics:** every human is the same white outline icon (circle head, rounded body) drawn at one line weight on black; the narrator's subject-self carries a four-point star on the chest, all other figures carry a small circle or nothing, so identity is tracked by a single glyph swap, never by faces or colour. Figures scale and reposition but never change design.
- **Why it works:** one reusable glyph vocabulary makes an 8-billion-person simulation and a single girl's story the same visual language; the star lets the viewer follow "the one I calculated" through crowds.
- **Evidence:** ev/001-bayes-board.jpg (star figure magnified out of a 40-icon array) · ev/133-simulation-panel.jpg (unmarked array) · ev/144-simulation-solo.jpg (star figure alone under the panel). Form: frame (visual claim).

### T02 — Probability board as the opening shot (formula first, story second)
- **Timestamps:** 0–13 s.
- **Mechanics:** the piece opens mid-diagram, not on a title: a targeting-reticle icon at top, the star figure inside a magnifier circle labelled (paraphrase — small text) "cause of failure no. 1", a 40-icon population array below, and the conditional-probability line P(F|H) ≈ 0.98 with its Bayes expansion typeset underneath. Elements draw on in layers over the first seconds while the voice states the thesis ("According to my calculations, the biggest cause of your failures, you humans, is hope").
- **Why it works:** the AI persona's authority is established by showing its native notation before any narrative — the viewer is inside the machine's worksheet from frame one.
- **Evidence:** ev/001-bayes-board.jpg (settled board) · ev/005-board-build-mid.jpg (board mid-build, population array drawing on) · clip clips/000-bayes-board-build.mp4 (0–12 s, the layered build). Form: frame + clip (build timing).

### T03 — The 2% bar chart (a number drawn as a height insult)
- **Timestamps:** ≈6–18 s.
- **Mechanics:** four tall empty bar outlines rise to a 100% axis; a tiny filled stub labelled ۲٪ (2%) appears at the base of the first bar while the star figure stands at the right at full height. The spoken contrast — "a thing has a 2% chance; I wait for the 2%; you wait for the 100%" (gloss) — lands on the visual disproportion.
- **Why it works:** the argument's central quantity is felt as a height ratio before it is understood as a statistic; the empty bars are the humans' expectation, drawn hollow.
- **Evidence:** ev/010-two-percent-bars.jpg. Form: frame.

### T04 — Calendar-of-days panel with crossed-out dates
- **Timestamps:** ≈13–27 s.
- **Mechanics:** a rounded panel labelled روزها ("Days") holds a grid of day numerals; most are struck through with diagonal slashes, two are ringed with a ⊗ mark, and dashed drop-lines connect two crossed dates to a timeline below where their labels are also struck out. A second panel (کارها, "Tasks") with empty checkboxes joins it. Draw-on order: grid → strikes → drop-lines → timeline labels.
- **Why it works:** "after such-and-such a date it will work out" is refuted visually — the calendar itself is a record of dates that already failed.
- **Evidence:** ev/020-calendar-days.jpg (calendar over the figure, "this time" arrow) · ev/026-calendar-tasks-crossout.jpg (both panels, crossed labels). Form: frame.

### T05 — Karaoke word-highlight captions as the transcript device
- **Timestamps:** throughout (bottom band).
- **Mechanics:** the creator's own burned-in Persian captions sit in a fixed bottom band; the currently spoken word renders in full white while the rest of the line sits dimmed, so the highlight sweeps the line in reading order, word by word, locked to the voice. The caption is the specimen's own — this packet adds no captions to any sample (production record, caption disposition).
- **Why it works:** in a faceless piece, the moving highlight is the persona's visible "speech act" — the eye is pulled to the exact word the machine is saying, which keeps a static diagram feeling spoken.
- **Evidence:** ev/066-house-scene.jpg (highlight mid-line) · ev/080-house-clock-verdict.jpg (highlight at line start) · clip clips/000-bayes-board-build.mp4 (sweep visible across lines). Form: frame + clip (sweep timing is the mechanic).

### T06 — Day-counter UI as section machinery

### T09 — Dual-line chart + report card (the metric that turns)
- **Timestamps:** ≈91–116 s.
- **Mechanics:** a line chart (y-axis تعداد, "count"; x-axis days 1–3) carries two labelled series: شروع‌ها ("starts", upper) and شکست‌ها ("failures", lower). First the failures line plunges to near zero at day 3 while a report card panel (گزارش · روز ۳) stamps "failures ↓ ✓"; then the starts line is seen to have plunged with it, the card's verdict lines accumulate ("failures ↓ ✓ · starts ↓ ? · fewer failures = fewer tries · simulation: stopped"), and a targeting reticle marks the narrator's attention shifting to the second number.
- **Why it works:** the reversal is performed by the persona's own instrument — the same chart that "proved" the thesis refutes it, so the AI is caught by its own evidence, not by a human rebuttal.
- **Evidence:** ev/093-flat-failure-line.jpg (chart early, failures line nearly flat across days 1–3 in the report window) · ev/100-failures-plunge.jpg (failures plunged, card stamped ✓, caption "I saw another number:") · ev/110-report-card-plunge.jpg (both lines down, full card) · clip clips/100-chart-plunge.mp4 (100–116 s). ### T10 — The rising probability curve (the fixed-probability error, corrected)
- **Timestamps:** ≈115–130 s.
- **Mechanics:** a new chart: y-axis احتمال قبولی ("probability of acceptance"), x-axis زمان ("time"). A dashed horizontal line sits at ۲٪ from امروز ("today"); from the same origin a solid curve bends upward, leaving the dashed line behind. Below, a report strip stamps "احتمال قبول ۲٪ ✓" — the old verdict — while the curve contradicts it.
- **Why it works:** the piece's thesis error is drawn as one wrong line: probability treated as a constant (dashed) against probability that grows as the person learns (curve). The whole philosophy of the episode is this pair of lines.
- **Evidence:** ev/120-probability-curve-rise.jpg. Form: frame.

### T11 — Simulation panel zoom-out and the dashed future self (verdict staging)

- **Mechanics:** the شبیه‌سازی ("Simulation") panel — a dense array of ≈120 small icons labelled ۸ میلیارد ("8 billion") — hangs above the single star figure; one icon in the array is bracketed as the selected case. - **Why it works:** dashed-vs-solid is the piece's whole metaphysics in one notation: the calculated present is solid and small; the hoped-for future is dashed and larger — and the final frame lets the dashed figure stand behind the solid one like a backing, not a fantasy.
