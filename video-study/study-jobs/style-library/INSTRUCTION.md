## Amendment A1 (2026-11-13, owner's recreation law — tier enforcement)
1. **Tiers:** every technique carries a tier — DESCRIBED (packet evidence only) · IN PROGRESS (recreation attempted, scores filed, below the bar) · RECREATED (passing recreation HTML on file, scores.md verdict PASS: average ≥85, no lens <70). Only RECREATED counts as a complete catalogue item.
