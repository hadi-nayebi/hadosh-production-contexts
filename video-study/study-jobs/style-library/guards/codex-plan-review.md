## 1. What it judges
- Deliverable: a samples-board plan or design produced by this job (the DESIGN draft, and later any build plan HQ charges from it) — judged against the owner's stated design for the board: technique-first browsing · frame thumbnails as evidence · filter by container · ten-word card hygiene · phone-first layout · tier on every line, only RECREATED printed as substrate.
- Consumer: the style-library cycle that produced the plan/design; the decision it prices is whether that face may be carried to HQ as meeting the owner's expectation.
- Call point: the Condense of the cycle that drafts or amends a board plan/design, and any later cycle that touches the board. Verdicts are advisory (2026-11-14 ruling): applied, or set aside with the miss recorded in that cycle's Condense.

## 2. Instruction (the review's charge)
Attack the board plan/design against the owner's stated design, in order:
1. EXACTLY AS ASKED? — does the design deliver technique-first browsing with packet evidence one tap away, or does it quietly become a packet shelf / a pretty index?
2. ANYTHING DIFFERENT? — every deviation from the §2 requirements in samples-board-DESIGN.md named, however small (a missing filter, a card field dropped, a view hand-maintained instead of derived).
3. ANY POINT REDUCED? — one pixel counts: card faces over the ten-word limit, tiers merged or hidden, thumbnails replaced by placeholders, phone layout demoted to a responsive afterthought, controls in the bottom-navigation zone.
Print the three answers as the return's first section; a YES to (2) or (3) names the cure or withdraws the claim that the design meets the expectation.
