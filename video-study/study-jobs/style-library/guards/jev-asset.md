# Jev call — jev-asset (style-library)

_Per-call definition from jev-call-template.md. Designed 2026-11-14. The judgment: ASSETS.md is a register of reusable lessons, not a highlights reel — this call is its gate._

## 1. What it judges
- Deliverable: a candidate asset (name · statement · evidence pointer · consumer line) at packet-close extraction, BEFORE it enters ASSETS.md.
- Call point: style-library INSTRUCTION §3, at the extraction step of a registration cycle.

## 2. Instruction (the model's charge)
Judge whether this candidate is a LESSON a future production can act on, or color from one packet wearing a lesson's clothes. Price: (1) REUSABILITY — is the statement phrased as a transferable principle/device another piece could deliberately use, with its conditions? (2) EVIDENCE BINDING — does it point at the packet evidence (timestamps/frames/counts) that teaches it? (3) CONSUMER NAMEDNESS — is a real consuming job/surface named, with the use it would make? (4) NON-DUPLICATION — is it distinct from assets already registered (a restatement is a duplicate even in new words)? Score 0–1 per dimension and classify defects. A true-but-useless observation fails here on purpose: the register's value is its density of usable lessons.

## 3. Context contract (masked view)
- FED: the candidate asset (all four fields) · current ASSETS.md names + statements (duplication check) · the packet's lessons-table rows the candidate derives from.
- NEVER FED: the extractor's rationale · the packet's reception data (popularity is not reusability).
- Ceiling: ≤32k tokens; candidates judged one at a time against the register.

## 4. Output space
- Verdict classes: **REGISTER · REGISTER-AS-VARIANT (folds under an existing asset as a named variant) · REJECT**.
- Dimensions (0–1): reusability · evidence binding · consumer namedness · non-duplication.
- Rejection taxonomy (one primary): PACKET-COLOR (true of this packet, transfers nowhere) · UNBOUND (no evidence pointer) · DUPLICATE · NO-CONSUMER.
- Accepted profiles travel: the profile line rides the ASSETS.md entry (consumers see the strength they are pulling).

## 5. Floors and disposition
- reusability < 0.6 → REJECT · consumer namedness < 0.5 → REJECT · non-duplication < 0.3 → REGISTER-AS-VARIANT or REJECT (never a silent second entry).
- Refusals print in the registration RUN with their class — an unregistered candidate is a recorded outcome, not a disappearance.
