## Reasoning

### 1. Reusability — 0.85
The statement is phrased as a deliberate device with its conditions, not as packet color. It names (a) the sustained condition — one flat palette register held across the whole piece so the world reads as one coherent place; (b) the exception budget — exactly one contrasting break; and (c) the placement rule — the break sits at the argument's turn. A future production can act on all three without reference to this specimen: pick one register, hold it, and spend the single contrast where the argument turns. The specimen's palette (deep blues with warm amber light sources) is correctly demoted to a parenthetical instance ("here: …") rather than stated as the lesson, and the transfer scope ("any illustrated explainer") is explicit. Held back from higher: "dusk break" still borrows the specimen's imagery for the general device — a plainer production phrasing would be "single contrasting palette break" — but the placement rule makes the intended meaning unambiguous, so this is a wording residue, not a transfer failure. This is not PACKET-COLOR: "the night scenes were blue and pretty" would be; a one-register-plus-one-break system with a turn-placement rule is not.

### 2. Evidence binding — 0.85
The pointer binds to checkable evidence at three levels: the packet's §3 catalogue technique P4-T23 (library T96), the §5.1 segmentation discussion, and three named frames (ev/t41.jpg, ev/t300.jpg, ev/t312.jpg) under a named on-disk directory, with timestamps "throughout" for the sustained register and a tight 4:58–5:05 window for the break. The frame set triangulates the claim — baseline register, break, and surrounding register — which is what a palette-sustainment claim requires; a single frame could not teach it. Held back from higher: the sustainment leg rests on "throughout" plus sampled frames rather than a counted frame census, so the binding proves the device at the sampled points and the break exactly, and asserts (plausibly, via §5.1) rather than enumerates the unbroken hold between them.

### 4. Non-duplication — 0.70
Checked against the full register, with closest attention to the three texture/system neighbours:

- **AS-04 — One material system across every element (texture / system):** shared abstraction — unity through one system held across elements. Distinct device: AS-04's system is material/physics (one substance forms words, pages, trees) and its unity is unbroken by design; it contains no exception and no placement rule. This candidate's system is chromatic (palette register), and its load-bearing second half — one deliberate break, spent once, at the argument's turn — has no counterpart in AS-04. A production can pull this candidate (palette + break) without pulling AS-04 (material), and vice versa.
- **AS-09 — Two-world color & type system (texture / system):** the closest neighbour, and the variant risk was priced seriously. AS-09 is an alternation system: two disciplined color worlds trade places throughout, and coherence across the variety is carried by ONE display face. This candidate is the inverse rhythm: ONE palette world sustained throughout, coherence carried by the palette itself, and contrast appearing exactly once as a turn-marker rather than as a second recurring world. Palette count (one vs two), rhythm (sustained + single exception vs alternation), unifier (palette vs typeface), and the function of contrast (mark the turn vs constitute a second world) all differ. Folding this under AS-09 as a variant would home a sustainment system inside an alternation asset and bury the single-break device where a consumer scanning AS-09 would not look for it. Related family, distinct asset — not a restatement in new words.
- **AS-21 — Status-tag artwork system (texture):** no substantive overlap. AS-21 tags artwork with epistemic status (measured / inferred / speculative) so honesty becomes visual; this candidate governs palette coherence and contrast placement and says nothing about status tagging. Sharing only the "(texture)" category label is not duplication.

Residual overlap with AS-04/AS-09 at the family level ("palette/system discipline produces coherence") is real, which is why this dimension scores 0.70 rather than higher — but the operative device differs from both, and 0.70 stands well above the §5 variant/reject floor of 0.30. No other registered asset (AS-01…AS-40) states a palette-register or single-break lesson.
