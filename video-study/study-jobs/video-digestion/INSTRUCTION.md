## §3 — Execute (the packet)
Build the packet directory `hidden_files/study/video-digests/<date>-<slug>/`:
1. **Archive + probe.** Save the source media (or record why it cannot be archived + the access route used). Probe it (duration, dimensions, fps, codecs, bitrate) into `probe.json`. Record the platform post data (author, date, caption, counts as displayed) into `post.json` — counts are reception raw material: copy them, do not interpret them here.
2. **Extract.** Frames per the §2 rule into `frames/`, named `<t_seconds>-<beat>.jpg`. Clips per the §2 clip rule into `clips/`, named likewise. No captions/subtitles are burned into any frame or clip, ever.
3. **Write the markdown twin** `digest.md` with exactly these sections:
   1. Specimen identity + technical facts (title, creator, platform, URL, date, duration, format, probe summary).
   2. Consumer + production surface (from §1.3) and how that consumer uses this packet in Observe.
   3. Technique catalogue — one entry per technique: **name · timestamps · mechanics** (what moves, in what order, eased how, timed to what) **· why it works · frame evidence** (frame filenames, cited inline).
   4. Tempo map — cuts/beats per section, pacing curve, where it accelerates/stalls; for long form, per-section.
   5. Goods and bads — honest pricing, both sides mandatory; a bad with no timestamp is an impression, not a finding.
   6. Lessons mapped to our containers — per lesson: container type (opening · transition · word animation · diagram · ending), the change it implies, build-cost note (cheap/moderate/expensive + why).
   7. Idea bank — producible variants for our lanes, each one line + the packet evidence it stands on.
   8. Reception link — pointer to the reception-analysis record for this specimen (or "not yet run").
4. **Render the PDF** `digest.pdf` from the same source content (the md twin is canonical; the PDF is its presentation form — they must not diverge; regenerate, never hand-edit one).
5. **Register the hand-off:** append the packet line to style-library's intake (its HQ.json entry + INDEX.md row is style-library's act; this job files the hand-off entry and cites the packet path).
Gate out: packet directory complete on disk; every catalogue entry cites ≥1 frame that exists.

## Amendment A3 (2026-11-14, the length protocol — the owner's direct ruling on his Rkb3IG46sQo send: "the video is long")
Specimen length is a priced property of every digestion, handled by rule, not rediscovered per packet:
1. **Transcript is length-immune:** the full timestamped transcript is produced at any runtime — it is cheap and a primary study object. Never sampled, never summarized in place of itself.
2. **Frame evidence scales by tier:** ≤5 min — full census as before (1fps + evidence set). >5 min — BEAT-LEVEL evidence: scene-change segmentation; evidence frames at every beat boundary + every cited technique moment; a sparse uniform net (1 frame / 10 s) as the only safety net. The packet's production record states the tier, the rule, and the counts.
3. **Length is priced as craft in every packet over 5 min** (category section + goods/bads): (a) what the length BUYS — beats/payoffs that only work given their runtime; (b) the SAG MAP — beats that repeat/stall/could be cut, each with its cut test (what would be lost); (c) the COMPRESSION FLOOR — the minimum runtime this same story survives at, which is the transfer bridge to short-form production (what a short in this category keeps and drops).
