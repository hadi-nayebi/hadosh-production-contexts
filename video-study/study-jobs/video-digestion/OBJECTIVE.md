## What this job is
The core job of the video study lane. Each cycle takes the head specimen from the lane queue (`../QUEUE.md`), archives and probes its media, extracts frames at motion beats (plus short cuts where a technique needs motion to be understood), and produces one **digest packet** — a PDF + a markdown twin — under `hidden_files/study/video-digests/<date>-<slug>/`. The packet digests style, motion-graphics techniques, goods and bads, qualities, and tempo; reception is priced by the sibling reception-analysis job and linked, never merged into the craft verdict.

## Success, stated so a cold reader can test it
A cycle succeeds when a production job (multilingual/DLC lane, studio, thumbnails), opening the packet cold during its Observe phase, can: (1) name the specimen and its technical facts in under a minute; (2) find each technique with its timestamps, mechanics, and frame evidence; (3) read an honest goods/bads pricing; (4) lift at least one lesson mapped to a named container (opening · transition · word animation · diagram) with a build-cost note; and (5) see the packet's named consumer and production surface on its face. A packet failing any of the five is not complete — it returns to Execute.

## Scope — in / out
- IN: media archiving + probing; frame/clip extraction; technique catalogue; tempo map; goods/bads; container-mapped lessons; idea bank of producible variants; packet registration hand-off to style-library.
- OUT: finding specimens (source-scouting owns the queue); engagement/reception analysis (reception-analysis owns it — this job links its verdict, never restates it as craft); publishing or rendering production videos; owner-facing delivery (routes through HQ).

## Shape decisions
- Grain: one specimen per cycle. A specimen needing two cycles (long-form, >3 min) splits by section at Plan — the split is recorded, never silently absorbed.
- Consumers named per packet, from: DLC/multilingual production (containers), studio lane (shots/styles), thumbnail job (packaging craft). At least one named consumer + surface per packet; "general inspiration" is not a consumer.
- Guards: in INSTRUCTION.md §6 (packet completeness census, frame-evidence binding, no-captions law, goods-and-bads presence). Guards are checklists executed and recorded per run until a mechanical checker is built — status printed honestly as UNBUILT where no command exists yet.

## Sealed
This objective changes only on HQ/owner word. The packet form (§3 of INSTRUCTION.md) changes only through a Condense amendment with a named specimen that proved the old form wrong.
