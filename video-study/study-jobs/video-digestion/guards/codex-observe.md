## Instruction (the Codex charge)
Attack this Observe as a context-gathering audit for a video-digestion cycle. Given the specimen's identity, its media properties, and the lane's prior-art citations as fed: (1) What does the established technique taxonomy / prior art for this specimen's form class (short beat-form vs long section-form) and style say the catalogue will need to distinguish — named techniques, confusable pairs, and mechanics vocabulary the digest must use precisely? (2) What prior art on this creator/style exists outside the lane record that the lane citations miss, and does any of it contradict the Observe framing? (3) For long-form specimens: what segmentation precedent applies (musical/visual section boundaries, tempo mapping), and what section signals should Observe have recorded before Plan chooses the model? (4) Which lesson classes (technique · trick · texture · storytelling pattern · transcript device · pacing principle) does prior art predict this specimen carries that the Observe naming may have missed? Return findings as priced gaps against Plan, each with its source; a finding with no source is an opinion and is labeled as one. Do not write the packet, do not choose the specimen, and do not price reception (reception-analysis owns it).
