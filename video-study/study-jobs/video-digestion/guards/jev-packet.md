## 2. Instruction (the model's charge)
Judge this packet as a cold production reader who must be able to USE it. Price, in order: (1) Does the packet structurally contain what its form promises — every required section present and substantive, not a heading with air under it? (2) Is every technique mechanic specific enough to rebuild from — what moves, when, by what construction — or is it impression prose? (3) Does every claim bind to evidence the packet itself supplies (timestamps, frame citations, counts)? (4) Are the goods AND the bads priced honestly — including what the packet could NOT evidence? (5) Is a real consumer named — a job + the surface it will use this on, such that the consumer could act — not merely a name dropped in? Score each dimension 0–1 against its anchors, classify every defect by the taxonomy (one primary class by precedence), and return the verdict the floors compose. You judge the packet's paperwork only; whether a cited frame truly shows its beat is a separate inspection gate and is NOT yours to certify. Class coverage is yours: under INSTRUCTION Amendment A4 clause 1, every lesson class the packet's form prices as present must appear in the lessons section tagged, or print its absence there — a class silent in both forms is a gap to classify under internal structural completeness, not a minor note. ## 4. Output space
- Verdict classes: **ADOPT · ADOPT-WITH-GAPS · REFUSE**. Per Run BC §3, ADOPT-WITH-GAPS is NARROW: a gap in required content BLOCKS adoption pending repair + rejudgment (the verdict for such a packet is REFUSE-side with the gap classified — Packet #1's G1–G3 would block, not ride along). ADOPT-WITH-GAPS is lawful only for a gap inside the designed nonblocking boundary (a named class of cosmetic/metadata gaps), with the downstream consequence recorded in the receipt.
- Dimensional scores (0–1; anchors: 0.0 = absent/false · 0.5 = present but partial or vague · 1.0 = complete and usable as-is):
  - internal structural completeness (renamed per Run BC — never "catalogue completeness" unless a source inventory was fed)
  - mechanics specificity
  - evidence binding (against supplied observations/text; image correspondence excluded — separate gate)
  - goods/bads honesty
  - consumer namedness (usable recipient + intended use, not name-presence — Run BC §2)
- Rejection taxonomy (one primary, by this precedence): MISSING-SECTION · NO-CONSUMER · UNBOUND-EVIDENCE · **OMITTED-SOURCE-TECHNIQUE** (a complete-looking catalogue that omits material the source inventory shows — Run BC §2; classifiable only when a source inventory was fed) · VAGUE-MECHANIC · RECEPTION-ABSENT.
- Accepted profiles travel downstream: the dimensional profile + receipt are cited by the adoption record and by style-library at registration (a packet's weak dimension is visible to its consumers — Run BC §3).
