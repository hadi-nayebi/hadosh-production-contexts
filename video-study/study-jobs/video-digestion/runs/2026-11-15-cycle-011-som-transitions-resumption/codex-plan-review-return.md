## FINDINGS

### Finding 1 — Segmentation collapses "motion beats" into the six demonstrations; the specimen's other motion events have no catalogue home
- **Condemned Plan line:** "**Segmentation model:** short form (80.99 s ≤ 90 s) → motion beats; the six demonstrations are the beat groups, each with its countdown lead-in. Decided from the landed probe, per Cycle-006's pre-registered conditional rule."
- **Class:** REVISE-IN-PLAN
- **Attack:** INSTRUCTION §2 defines short-form segmentation as motion beats where "every distinct motion event is a candidate catalogue entry." §1's own viewing record names motion events outside the six demos: the opening logo/pencil/timeline-dive (0–12 s), the definition card (12 s), the complexity ladder build (14–17 s), the logo outro (59–62 s), and the tutorial's own devices (countdown pre-announcement, cursor-follow zoom) which §1 prices as technique/trick/transcript-device present. The Plan's beat-group model silently demotes all of these to non-entries. Beat-first segmentation would produce a materially different catalogue (opening dive, ladder build, and countdown as entries in their own right); section/group-first produces exactly six. The Plan never prices that difference, and the ambiguity propagates: §10 reporting requires a technique count, and this Plan cannot say whether the count is 6 or 6 + devices.
- **Cure:** Amend §2 with an explicit beat inventory: every §1 motion event listed and disposed as (a) catalogue entry, (b) lessons-only asset, or (c) excluded with reason. State the resulting technique count the packet will carry.

### Finding 2 — Demo boundaries are article/clock-derived approximations, not signal-derived; the probe's scene signals contradict them and are never reconciled
- **Condemned Plan line:** "**Segmentation model:** short form (80.99 s ≤ 90 s) → motion beats; the six demonstrations are the beat groups, each with its countdown lead-in. Decided from the landed probe, per Cycle-006's pre-registered conditional rule."
- **Class:** REVISE-IN-PLAN
- **Attack:** §1 records scene-change signals at 1.9 · 6.3 · 32.1 · 44.0 · 45.7 s — five signals for six claimed demonstrations whose §1 boundaries are all approximate ("hard cut ~17–22 s · dissolve ~23–30 s · cut on action ~30–36 s · match cut ~37–44 s · dynamic zoom ~45–52 s · morph ~53–59 s"). Most demo cuts land inside a reused flat two-scene system (§1 texture pricing), so scene detection structurally cannot see them. The Plan claims the model was "decided from the landed probe," but the boundaries actually come from the companion article's taxonomy plus viewing impression. §3.3(3) requires per-entry timestamps; the Plan charges no step that converts the "~" ranges into measured cut timestamps from the 2 fps grid or the clips. Execute will inherit approximate timestamps and either ship them (a tempo/evidence defect) or improvise the measurement unplanned.
- **Cure:** Add an Execute step, before digest writing, that measures each demonstration's exact in/out timestamps from the grid frames (and clips where they exist), records them in RUN.md, and reconciles them against the five probe scene signals with the flat-scene explanation stated.

### Finding 3 — Clip rule is asserted for two techniques only; no per-entry stills-sufficiency ruling for the other four, two of which are inherently temporal
- **Condemned Plan line:** "**Extraction plan:** inherited extraction stands (grid 2 fps · ev 18 · clips 2 — match cut + morph, the two techniques stills cannot carry, exactly Cycle-006's pre-named clip candidates)."
- **Class:** EXECUTE-DEFECT
- **Attack:** The guard charge requires the clip rule to fire "only where stills cannot carry a technique — named per entry, not by habit." The Plan names the two positive cases and gives no ruling at all for hard cut, dissolve, cut on action, or dynamic zoom. Two of the unruled four are continuous-motion techniques: a dissolve is a timed blend (a single frame shows a blend state, not its duration or easing) and a dynamic zoom is continuous scale motion (stills show endpoints, not the zoom's speed curve — the very mechanic the entry must teach). Cut on action's mechanic is the cut's placement inside a movement, likewise temporal. As planned, Execute will build dissolve and dynamic-zoom entries on stills alone, and Verify's evidence-binding check (frame shows the claimed beat) cannot catch it, because a blend/zoom frame superficially "shows" the technique while carrying none of its timing.
- **Cure:** Add a per-entry clip table to §2: for each of the six techniques, clip / no-clip with the stills-sufficiency reason in one line. If dissolve, cut on action, or dynamic zoom are ruled clip-needed, name the extraction in the Plan (timestamps + duration) rather than leaving it to Execute improvisation; if ruled stills-sufficient, the reason must state how duration/easing claims in those entries will be bound (e.g., grid-frame sequence with timestamps, cited as a sequence per Amendment A4 clause 2).

### Finding 4 — The before/during/after frame rule is never mapped onto the inherited 18 ev frames, and the Execute checklist's evidence bar is weaker than the rule
- **Condemned Plan line:** "3. ☐ Write digest.md (all eight §3.3 sections; every catalogue entry cites ≥1 ev frame that exists; stated counts re-counted from disk at close per Amendment A5)."
- **Class:** EXECUTE-DEFECT
- **Attack:** INSTRUCTION §2's frame rule is one frame per technique's before/during/after, plus every motion beat and section boundary. Eighteen ev frames across six techniques is exactly 3 per technique only if evenly distributed — the Plan contains no per-entry ev-coverage table, so nobody has checked that distribution, and the inherited set was extracted in Cycle-006, not against this Plan. Worse, Execute checklist step 3 sets the pass bar at "≥1 ev frame that exists" per entry — one third of the §2 rule. A packet can clear the checklist as written with single-frame entries that violate the Plan's own sampling rule, and the Verify checklist's census (citations resolve to files) checks existence, not B/D/A completeness.
- **Cure:** Add to §2 a per-technique evidence map (ev filenames × before/during/after + boundary) built from the on-disk ev set; where a cell is empty, name the added extraction. Raise step 3's bar to match the map, and add B/D/A completeness as a named Verify property with the map as its instrument.

### Finding 5 — Tempo-map method cannot support tempo claims: no counting method, no thresholds, no transition durations
- **Condemned Plan line:** "(4) tempo map ← section/beat timings from the viewing record"
- **Class:** REVISE-IN-PLAN
- **Attack:** "Section/beat timings from the viewing record" yields segment lengths only — impressions with numbers attached. The guard's standard is Packet #1: counts at stated thresholds (25 hard cuts at 0.35 vs 48 at 0.22 in 198 s) plus intra-shot animation density as the energy measure a cut-count alone would miss. This Plan states no scene-change threshold, no cut-count method (and Finding 2 shows the probe's automatic signals undercount this specimen's cuts by design), no per-section cut/beat counts, and — most damaging for a transitions tutorial — no measurement of transition durations themselves (dissolve length, zoom duration, morph duration), which are this specimen's tempo facts. It also never charges measuring the countdown intervals, so §1's "countdown metronome" pacing claim will enter the packet unmeasured. The §2 failure-point menu item "tempo-without-timestamps" is not evaluated anywhere in the Plan's failure-point line.
- **Cure:** Specify the tempo method in §2: manual cut count from the grid per beat group (with the probe signals as cross-check, discrepancy explained), transition duration per catalogue entry in seconds, countdown interval per demonstration, and the 62–81 s end card carried as an explicit zero-motion segment. State the counts the tempo map will print before Execute begins.

### Finding 6 — The sampling-adequacy claim prices the countdown but not the cut moments the specimen's core mechanics depend on
- **Condemned Plan line:** "sampling gaps — countdown states are 1 s apart, grid is 2 fps: adequate"
- **Class:** REVISE-IN-PLAN
- **Attack:** 2 fps (one frame per 0.5 s) is adequate for 1 s countdown states, but the specimen's signature mechanics are sub-second events: the match cut lands "at their fastest movement" (queue line, confirmed by the article), and cut on action turns on the exact frame the cut falls inside the action. A 0.5 s grid can miss the fastest-movement frame entirely. The match cut is partially covered by its clip; cut on action has no clip (Finding 3) and no denser sampling is planned around either cut point. The blanket "adequate" verdict prices the easiest sampling demand in the specimen and skips the hardest.
- **Cure:** Price sampling per mechanic, not per specimen: name the two cut points (match cut, cut on action) and the evidence that binds each (clip frame range / ev sequence), or add targeted denser frame extraction around those timestamps to the extraction plan.

### Finding 7 — Goods/bads (G1) is planned as a packet section but not as a production step, and the one bad already assigned to it is not a specimen bad

- **Cure:** Add an explicit Execute step: price goods/bads against the viewing record before writing §3.3(5), with at least the end-card bad carried by timestamp and each demonstration open to a priced bad; move the D1 transcript limitation to the packet's honesty/limitation slot only, and state in §2 that §3.3(5) prices the specimen, not the digestion.
