**Finding 1 — REVISE-IN-PLAN — S3 is a category error: an interleaved event type is filed as a sequential section, so the S1–S4 spine is section-first in disguise.**
- Condemned Plan line: "- **Segmentation model:** SHORT FORM (60 s ≤ 90 s) → motion beats. Beat = word onset (RSVP replacement at a fixed position). - Defect: S1, S2 and S4 are sequential phases of one stream; S3 is not — by the line's own words the flashes are "inside the word stream," i.e. they interrupt S2 (and possibly S1/S4) repeatedly. A tempo map that aggregates "per section" cannot assign a flash that occurs mid-acceleration to a separate S3 block without either double-counting its time or breaking S2's rate series at every interruption and re-joining it silently. This is exactly where section-first vs beat-first produces a materially different catalogue here: beat-first (word onsets + flash events on one timeline, sections derived afterwards from rate bands) yields flash-as-punctuation entries; the filed spine yields a phantom "intercut section" and section wpm figures whose denominators are undefined. P-A3's re-derivation step (Execute step 4) mitigates the boundary placement but does not cure the type error, because the spine it re-derives still contains S3 as a section.
- Cure: Strike S3 from the section spine in the operative line. Model flashes as an event overlay on the single onset timeline (flash onset/offset rows, per P-A7), and let the section list contain only sequential phases; the flash pattern (frequency, placement at phrase boundaries, ramp behaviour across each flash) is catalogued as a technique, not a section. Print the revised spine in §2, not only in an amendment.
