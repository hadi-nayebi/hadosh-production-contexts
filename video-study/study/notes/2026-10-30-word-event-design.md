## Decomposition first (per the 2026-10-30 decomposition-first rule)

A word event is made of: (1) selection — which spoken words earn an event at all;
(2) function — what cueing job the event does; (3) dosage — how much expressive
treatment, in what unit; (4) typographic grammar — face / weight / scale / colour as
meaning; (5) motion grammar — direction/mode and what that motion *means*;
(6) sync — entry timing against the spoken anchor, with a tolerance; (7) residence —
dwell against reading speed, co-visibility, exit; (8) object form — when a word stops
being type and becomes an image asset; (9) interference — against scene, labels, and
other events (Oneness Q-c); (10) verification — plan-side forecast vs the delivered-file
floors in decision-model §23. The session studied each part against the best method
found for it, in the curriculum's trust order.

## Question the session set out to answer

The curriculum phrases this topic as "kinetic typography at 10–20% dosage." Is that
phrase a design rule a planner can actually use — and what is missing between the
model's existing rows (§9 per-word containers, §9A kinetic-type events) and its
delivered-file gates (§23 coverage / motion / presence floors)?

**Findings from our own evidence:**

- **F1 — "Dosage" conflates three different quantities.** (i) *Style intensity*:
  Hadi's taste calibration against the extreme reference (below) — not measurable.
  (ii) *Expressive share*: expressive events ÷ all events (soa-01: 7/34 ≈ 20%).
  (iii) *The §23 measured index*: coverage / motion / presence on the delivered file.
  The curriculum's "10–20% dosage" phrase mixes (i) and (ii); §23 already separated
  (iii). Any plan that reports one "dosage" number is reporting none of them.
- **F2 — Direction choice has no stated semantics in our best plan.** §7 assigns
  RISE/FALL/SLIDE/GROW/STAMP per event, but no mapping says what a direction *means*,
  so the same motion can carry opposite claims in one film and nothing audits it.
  Brownie's taxonomy (below) is exactly this distinction: motion vs transformation
  are different kinetic behaviours, chosen per meaning.
- **F3 — No sync tolerance exists for word entries.** Scene switches carry ±0.15 s
  (§10C.8); word-event entry "starting on the anchor" carries no tolerance and no
  measurement method in the model (§11 M24 is motion-general, not word-specific).
- **F4 — No plan→gate bridge.** §23 is measured after render; nothing requires the
  plan to *forecast* coverage and presence from its own event table before build,
  so a below-floor word layer is discovered as a delivery failure instead of being
  impossible by construction (the guarantee-by-construction rule).

### 3. Proven research / creator craft (sources, read directly)

- **De Koning, Tabbers, Rikers & Paas (2009), "Towards a Framework for Attention
  Cueing in Instructional Animations," *Educational Psychology Review*** (Springer,
  abstract + framework read) — cueing has three functions: **selection** (guide
  attention to a location/element), **organization** (emphasise structure), and
  **integration** (explicate relations between elements). Evidence: selection cueing
  facilitates attention and sometimes learning; organizational and relational cueing
  "requires more consideration." Direct implication: most word events are selection
  cues and should be designed as such; an event claiming organization must actually
  show structure (list accumulation, contrast split — soa-01's E4/E16 persist sets
  and E13 contrast pair do); an event claiming integration must enact the relation
  (a word travelling to its referent, or becoming the object — E9 TRAVERSE, E17
  YOURS) — expressive motion alone does not integrate anything.
- **Signaling meta-analyses** — Alpizar, Adesope & Wong (2020, *ETR&D*: 29 studies,
  44 effect sizes, N=2726): signaling d = 0.38, p < .01; Schneider et al. (2018)
  converge, with reduced perceived cognitive load; Mayer (2017) summary: signaling
  d = 0.46, temporal contiguity d = 1.30. Two implications: (a) highlighting works
  when it is *selective* — a signal applied to everything signals nothing, which is
  the research form of our calm-majority / expressive-minority split; (b) the
  temporal-contiguity effect is the largest in the set — sync is not polish, it is
  the strongest single variable, which justifies giving word entries a hard
  tolerance (F3) rather than a style note.
- **Brownie, *Transforming Type* (2014)** (taxonomy via the book's published
  structure and the secondary literature quoting it) — kinetic typography divides
  into *motion typography* (scrolling; dynamic layout — elements move in relation
  to each other / the frame) and *fluid typography* (letterforms themselves change).
  Our direction set is motion typography; SWELL/STAMP trajectories border on fluid.
  The useful discipline: name which behaviour class an event uses and why — a
  transformation claims the word itself changes state; a relocation claims its
  place/relation changes. Mixing them without that reason is F2's defect in
  theoretical form.
- **Practitioner caption/kinetic systems (lower trust, used only where they
  converge with the above)** — Cursa's caption-systems guide: caption the *payload*,
  not every word; pick 2–3 animation behaviours and assign each a meaning;
  readability math ≈12–15 characters/second for mobile, minimum on-screen time
  0.8–1.2 s for short phrases, numbers held +0.2–0.5 s longer. The 2–3 behaviour
  rule converges with soa-01's closed direction set + 3-face grammar (a small,
  consistent vocabulary, repeated) and with the coherence principle from session 1.
  The readability figures are adopted below as a *provisional* floor — marked as
  such, to be re-derived from our own films like §23.10's calibration discipline —
  because G-03 has stood at class D with no floor at all.

## Synthesis — what changes

1. **Dosage is three numbers, reported separately, forever:** style intensity
   (taste statement vs the named reference), expressive share (expressive events ÷
   all events; soa-01 precedent ≈20%, working band 15–25% with reasons outside it),
   and the §23 measured triple on the delivered file. No single "dosage" figure.
2. **Every event carries a cue function** — SELECTION (default), ORGANIZATION
   (structure must be visible: accumulation / contrast / list), or INTEGRATION
   (relation must be enacted: travel-to-referent / word-becomes-object). An
   expressive treatment on a pure selection event is decoration and is flagged.
3. **Selection grammar by semantic role:** verdict/definition, naming, number/scale,
   contrast pair, custody/ownership turn, mantra imperative earn events first;
   setup/function stretches are calm or foreclosed *with the reason recorded*
   (soa-01 L7 precedent). This replaces both rationing and "every word performs."
4. **A per-piece motion-meaning table is mandatory:** each direction/mode in the
   piece's palette is assigned the meaning it carries in *that* piece and reused
   consistently; the same motion carrying opposite meanings in one piece is a defect.
5. **Sync tolerance for word entries: ±0.15 s** from the measured spoken anchor
   (words.json), the same tolerance as scene switches (§10C.8) — justified by the
   temporal-contiguity evidence, the strongest effect in the signaling literature.
6. **A provisional reading-dwell floor closes G-03's core:** settled dwell
   (post-entry, pre-exit) ≥ max(0.8 s, characters ÷ 15 per second); events carrying
   a number/quantity get +0.3 s; PERSIST/HOLD events are measured cumulatively.
   Provisional, practitioner-sourced, re-derived when our accepted set grows.
7. **Plan-side forecast of §23:** before build, the plan computes forecast coverage
   (event words ÷ transcript words) and forecast presence (union of event windows
   ÷ runtime) from its own event table; a forecast below the §23 floor is a plan
   defect, fixed in the plan — guarantee by construction, not a post-render surprise.
8. **Image-asset word criteria (not a quota):** a word earns object form when
   (a) its claim is an object/custody claim, (b) the object form depicts that claim,
   (c) its own contrast is decided (soa-01 E17: no scrim, by recorded decision).
   Scarcity comes from the criteria, honouring the retired-rationing doctrine.
