## Findings
- **Canon has a concept layer and no visual layer.** canon.md locks what the parts ARE and never how anything LOOKS. The palette's hex values live in a container spec; the figure law lives in MEMORY and a research file; QC V-01 is one line. A builder deriving a style "from canon" today assembles it from at least four files — or, as the anatomy lane did, from none.
- **Colour has roles in the poster that no file states.** Reading the poster as the spec it is: CYAN = the digital layer itself (network outline, compartment boxes — the harness made visible); AMBER/GOLD = the points of light on the network (energy, messages, attention — the same amber that carries the container's subscribe/payoff accents); CREAM CHALK = the human and the headings (the organic, the authored); CHARCOAL = the ground everything stands on. A plate that colours the human cyan or the network cream has broken canon even if its hexes are "in palette" — §2.6 counts a palette as a set, never as roles.
- **The hero reference is a live trap.** It is faced, photographic, and multi-hue — three direct contradictions of decided law (faceless ruling; no photos in shorts; cyan+amber discipline) — and it is the most findable "Digital Cortex" image in `files/`. The 2026-10-31 study reclassified it in a research file a builder may never open. The reclassification must live in canon, where style is derived.
- **Text-in-image has no rule, and our canon makes text unusually load-bearing.** The compartment labels ARE canon terms (LLMs plural, per the terminology ledger); a generated plate that misspells or invents a label is not a style blemish, it is a canon-fidelity defect (§4.4). External evidence and our own HTML-scene practice agree: exact text belongs in the deterministic layer (composited labels, the word layer), generated plates are generated textless. Hadi's own poster is the exception that proves the boundary — it is HIS supplied artwork, used as itself, never a licence to ask a model for lettering.
- **Subject fidelity is a reference discipline, not a prompt talent.** §10A already requires a master file per asset; nothing requires the per-scene generation (§10.2) to actually USE it — verbatim style block + subject anchor + master reference attached, scene as the only variable. That missing link is the mechanism behind "images are random": each scene re-rolled the subject and the style together.
