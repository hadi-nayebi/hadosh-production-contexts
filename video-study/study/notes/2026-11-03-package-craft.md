  recorded), settings audit (4 of 13 fields), cover (frame pick only, no

  standard would demand a Shorts cover and Shorts settings that do not
  apply. The standard's scope line (Shorts only; community posts under
  `files/checklists/community-post.md`, long-form under its own checklist)
  is therefore load-bearing and is written into the file's head.

  at a future publish, a standard demanding the upload would block a
  publish. The standard therefore requires *verify eligibility, upload if
  eligible, else frame-pick fallback recorded* — the HLj package's own form.
