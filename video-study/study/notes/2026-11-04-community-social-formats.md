### 3–4. Platform guidance (YouTube Help, read in full 2026-11-04)

- **Create a post** (support.google.com/youtube/answer/7124474, Desktop):
  image/GIF posts — **up to 10 images, ≤16 MB each, JPG/PNG/GIF/WEBP, 1:1
  suggested** (shown that way in feed; expandable); **text polls — up to 4
  answers, ≤65 chars each**; **image polls — up to 4 images, captions ≤36
  chars**; **quizzes — up to 4 answers, ≤80 chars each (≤30 if an image is
  added), exactly ONE correct answer, optional explanation ≤500 chars;
  quizzes cannot be edited once saved — delete and recreate**; **playlist
  posts** exist (paste a playlist URL); **video posts** — a post about a video
  already in a viewer's Subscription/Home feed **might not show again**;
  posts can be **scheduled**; a **24-hour post cap** exists. Text posts can't
  be combined with polls.
- **Learn about posts** (answer/9409631): posts surface on the channel Posts
  shelf/tab, viewers' Home and Subscriptions feeds, and **image posts may
  additionally show in the Shorts feed**; subscribers may be notified; video
  posts **inherit the shared video's comment settings**.
- iOS variant of Create a post (via search result, labelled as such): image
  posts can add **music up to 15 s** in the app, which plays in the Shorts
  feed; **music cannot be added or edited after posting**.
