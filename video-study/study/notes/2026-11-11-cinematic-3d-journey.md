## The decomposed journey grammar (what the conversion must carry)

1. Camera path as narrative: each flight segment serves one narration beat; the move's story reason is named per segment (follow, reveal, emphasise, transit). An unnamed move is an unmotivated move.
2. Through-line object: one thing, followed end-to-end, with a written transformation chain; identity survives every form change; the object is present in every segment.
3. Scale changes as scene switches: the plan carries a scale ladder (hand → inside the data → city → ocean floor → globe in the reference); each rung change is a scene switch and a STRONG event boundary.
4. Dwell at story beats: transit is fast, payoff beats dwell — the flight slows or holds where the learning lands (the reference dwells on the cable cross-section and the reassembly). Dwell is an emphasis budget, per section 10C's doctrine, applied to camera speed instead of scene length.
5. HUD spine: a persistent counter (the ms clock) is the piece's own measured quantity, growing monotonically; it is cast, not decoration.
6. Numbers as actors + labels on objects: big numbers perform (split, tumble, reassemble); documentary labels ride their objects through space; all exact text composited deterministically per canon section 7, and NO caption layer (Hadi's law overrides the reference's form here).
7. World moves past: parallax and depth do the animation work; the frame never freezes; presenter inserts, if any, are decided cuts, not defaults.
8. Lane discipline: journey is a second lane, not a reskin — palette declared per piece, one style per piece, and a true-3D build enters specimen-first on Hadi's word only. The 2D-stealable principles (follow one thing, growing counter, numbers as actors, attached labels, world-moves-past) remain available to scenes+words pieces without importing the lane.
