## The decomposed diorama grammar (what the conversion must carry)

1. One idea per shot: the shot list IS the idea list — each shot carries one mechanism step, joined by cuts; shot boundaries are idea boundaries.
2. Camera observes from outside: static, carefully composed shots; inside a shot, motion is slow and gentle (object turning, exploded view separating); the camera never flies through a world — the inverse of the journey lane.
3. Invisible → physical (the signature move): every abstract quantity becomes geometry with a written mapping — distance = wireframe sphere intersecting the Earth; clock error = ~300 m red circle + drifting dot; relativity = two clock readouts linked by the same line; a phone's clock = the phone exploded into layers. An abstraction left narration-only fails the form.
4. Hero-object anchor: each episode carries one detailed hero (satellite cutaway, exploded phone); supporting geometry stays simplified — detail is spent where the idea lives.
5. Lane palette and materials: pale grey / off-white / pale blue daylight; matte simplified geometry, smooth shading, soft shadows; one restrained accent system (red/orange for signals, spheres, errors; blue for the location dot); bright and diagrammatic, never dark-cinematic.
6. Quiet typography, reconciled: in-scene diagram labels in one restrained system, composited deterministically; honesty labels ("not to scale", "illustrative") are furniture — required wherever a simplification could mislead, never fine print. The caption pill is NOT adopted (Hadi's law).
7. Unhurried motion: diagram animation at a calm tempo — nothing freezes, nothing rushes; one movement per shot, tied to that shot's idea.
8. Calm sound: one measured narrator; music/effects subordinate; tone matches the visuals exactly.
9. Playlist shape: fixed series furniture (palette, label system, hero grammar, honesty labels, voice) re-skinned per episode with a new mechanism — consistency IS the brand; natural home is a "how X actually works" playlist (Earth One technology / Observations pieces).
10. Lane discipline: as true 3D it is Blender-class and enters specimen-first, judged on style alone, only on Hadi's word — and whether it becomes a playlist template is his open decision, not this session's. The stealable principles (one idea per shot · invisible-made-physical · honesty labels · fixed series furniture) port into 2D / scenes+words pieces without importing the lane.
