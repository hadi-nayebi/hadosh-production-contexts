## Findings

- **F1 — Two standards claim the joint slots, neither scopes itself.**
  §10C.1 sets a per-piece dwell ceiling of 5.0 s; an assembler container
  (a language repeat, a diagram) runs far past that by configuration.
  §10C.7 forecloses every transition type but two crossfades; the
  owner's spec requires transition containers at the joints. Neither
  text names the other's form. An unacknowledged contradiction is a
  join gap (rival-claim leg).
- **F2 — The literal readings both fail.** Applied to an assembler piece,
  §10C manufactures false defects (every container a ceiling breach;
  every transition block banned). Dropped for assembler pieces, §10C
  takes its anchor grading with it — and container joints are precisely
  where STRONG flips (new container type, new language role, verdict /
  payoff joints) concentrate. The grading is the part of §10C the new
  form needs most.
- **F3 — Inside its own form, §10C stands.** Nothing in the assembler
  record contradicts graded anchors, the dwell band, or crossfade-only
  scene switches inside a scenes+words piece or inside one container.
  This is a scope defect, not a substance defect — the cure is a scope
  statement plus the inter-container rows, not a rewrite.

- **R9 — counterexamples:** (1) a 2.8 s scene switch inside a diagram

  the new grain; (3) a crossfade claimed as a "transition container"
  fails 10C.10 — a fade is not a container; (4) a scenes+words piece
  claiming assembler scope to escape the 5.0 s ceiling fails the scope
