NEUTRAL TECHNIQUE FACTS
Technique: dynamic / infinite zoom. The camera follows the cursor into a laptop screen and through it into a webpage scene - a continuous scale move with no cut; the incoming scene is a sub-frame of the outgoing one, nested inside it from the start, so scale replaces the cut as the continuity device and the cursor gives the viewer a guide to follow.
Measured segment facts (segment = source 43-57 s, 14.000 s; segment time = source time - 43 s; census frames at 0.25 s steps): at segment 7.50 the laptop is small and centred and the cursor approaches from the right, the screen blank teal; at 7.75 the push is underway; at 8.00 the laptop is close and the white cursor is on the teal screen; at 8.25 teal fills the frame, the cursor is still visible, and a small yellow seed dot sits at centre; at 8.50-9.00 the search pill grows and its text types in; the arrived webpage state holds at 9.50-9.75; the settled composition (pill complete, first result card present) stands at 10.00.
Timing bases in dispute (both printed; judge which the build honours): Basis A - a retained builder basis: camera travel bound as an allocation of 45% of the 2.6 s transition span (1.170 s), from a coarser estimate of about 1.1 s of travel. Basis B - a segment-evidence basis: quarter-second census brackets bound travel between 0.25 s and 0.75 s; the endpoint reading (distant at 7.50, full teal at 8.25) gives 0.75 s, a fraction of 750/2600 (about 0.288) of the span. The build's cycle is 14 s: a 1.4 s distant hold, a 2.6 s transition window, and a 10.0 s arrived hold (the durations are allocations; the transition window's internal split is what the bases dispute).
Construction: one self-contained HTML file; all geometry, text, palette and timing derive from a CONFIG block at the top of the script, with a frozen defaults block used only as validation fallback; no external assets; no specimen media.
