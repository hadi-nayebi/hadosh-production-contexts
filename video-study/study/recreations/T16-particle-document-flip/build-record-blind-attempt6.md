WHAT THE BUILD IS
A single self-contained HTML file. A stack of document pages, rendered as particles sampled from config-rendered text and shapes, flips through its page counter (pages 1 to 14 at counter checkpoints 1, 6 and 14, at 0 ms, 1500 ms and 2200 ms of a 6000 ms cycle); the finished stack disperses, a title condenses out of the particle stream with a ring above it, the title holds, then disperses inside a dedicated seam interval (5700-6000 ms) and the cycle restarts with no borrowed particles carried across the seam. Phases: flip [0,2600), release [2600,3100), form [3100,4600), hold [4600,5700), seam [5700,6000). Page intervals: 300 ms early, 87.5 ms rapid, and a 400 ms final-page hold. The build starts its own animation loop on load.

CONSTRUCTION FACTS
- Two faces per page turn: each page carries a persistent face (its outline and header) and an incoming face (its line texture). During a turn the persistent face folds around the page's left-edge pivot with an asymmetric profile — it deepens over the first part of the turn window to a horizontal factor of −0.12 (the far edge crosses the pivot), holds briefly, and returns to full width by 70% of the turn window; the incoming face unfolds from the pivot across the same window, its factor rising from the configured minimum factor to 1.0 by the same 70% point. Both faces are therefore at full width for the final 30% of every turn window.
- Turn: every page change restarts the page-turn animation; its effective duration is derived from the interval it must fit (the configured turn duration, capped so a settled dwell remains inside the interval). The turn window, and every travel and release the change triggers, opens at the change's SCHEDULED instant, not at the tick that detects it; the same scheduled-instant anchoring applies to the first page's borrow (cycle start), the release phase (its scheduled start), formation borrows (the scheduled formation start; ring borrows at formation start plus the configured ring delay) and the seam release.
- Readability predicates (two, both per-particle): the strict predicate requires settled, turn window complete, and registration complete. The geometric predicate requires settled and registration complete with the particle's own face at a horizontal factor of at least 0.98. A page's dwell under a predicate counts the time every borrowed particle of the page satisfies it.
- Registration: after arrival, each particle's structure registers over a per-particle duration derived from the span remaining inside its turn window (delay + travel subtracted, capped so delay + travel + registration completes by 70% of the turn window). Formation registration is derived the same way against a scheduler-set deadline — the hold entry — so title and ring registration complete by the hold entry; the build measures and records the resolved hold (hold measured from actual registration completion): at the defaults both title and ring resolve at the hold entry and the resolved hold equals the full nominal hold of 1100 ms.
- Budget and formation feasibility price the COMPLETE particle lifecycle: the schedule's per-interval need includes arrival plus the span-derived page registration, and formation need includes the configured registration window and the frame cap. Enforcement re-checks after every fallback restore and restores every schedule dependency the fallback touched before re-checking; a combination that cannot hold is refused (defaults restored, warning recorded) rather than run.
- Identity: page targets carry stable keys; line targets key on their page line index and position, so a line target keeps its identity across page changes. Assignment completeness at the defaults is total (zero unassigned targets across full cycles, including stepped rollovers across the cycle seam at four phase offsets and two cadences).
- Releases: a release starts from the particle's actual rendered position with its rendered velocity captured, blends that derivative out over a configured fraction of the release window, and eases to rest at a stream point; there is no per-release random term. A phase release with no explicit duration is capped at the release window it must complete inside; the particle pool is priced against the scatter duration that can actually occur concurrently, not the nominal one. A particle whose release has not completed in scheduled time cannot be borrowed.
- Formation assignment is assignment-truthful: a formation flag is set only when its borrow assigned every target; a short borrow records its shortfall in diagnostics and retries on later ticks while its window remains. At the defaults the title assigns 6194/6194 targets and the ring 289/289, at both the configured release window and a 50 ms release window.
- Counter: the page counter (label + numeral) is fitted as one group against the space right of the page/stack region; the numeral font has a configured floor, and if the group cannot fit beside the page at that floor it moves to a fallback position above the page. At a 360 px stage the fitted numeral is 34 px.
- Intensity: the configuration's intensity values are validated JOINTLY as a hierarchy — surplus < reserve < travel < stack < foreground, with ring intensity at most the foreground intensity. An inverted pair is corrected field by field (the offending side resets to its default with a warning and the pair is re-checked); a set still violating after correction is refused wholesale. A particle's alpha interpolates from its travel intensity to its settled intensity as its registration progresses.
- Validation: configuration is validated for finiteness, ranges and text content; every numeric key survives a non-finite and magnitude sweep (NaN, ±Infinity, 9.99e300, a 400-digit value) with no throw and a finite value after validation — phase durations carry an explicit magnitude bound because the schedule scan is proportional to the flip window. Joint relationships (sampling-gap order, halo extent, checkpoint ordering, counter/region ratios, budget and formation feasibility) are re-checked after clamping; unsatisfiable combinations are refused wholesale with a warning.
- Text fitting: title and header are fitted in width and height using measured ink bounds; the ink width is the sum of the measure's left and right ink extents (the full ink width for glyphs whose ink overhangs the origin asymmetrically). Fitted results are asserted as POSITIONS, not only sizes: the title's ink box lies inside the stage and inside its fitted box; the header's ink box top sits exactly at the page top plus the configured header offset, its height equals the fitted height, and it lies inside the page box. Every policy value the fitting and timing logic consumes (registration floor, turn-profile ramp fraction, release blend fraction, frame cap, fold depth) is a configuration key.

- PIXEL-UNVERIFIED: no rendered-pixel verification was available in the build environment; all geometry evidence is from the deterministic model harness summarised below.
- Reading dwell on rapid pages: under the strict predicate (settled, turn complete, registration complete, every borrowed particle), the measured readable dwell is 16.7 ms per rapid page at 60 fps sampling; under the geometric predicate it is 16.7–33.3 ms per rapid page. No longer readable dwell is claimed for rapid pages.
- Sampled readable frames at coarse sampling: at 50 ms sampling steps, 0 of the 8 rapid pages show a fully readable frame under the page-level (every-particle) criterion; at 60 fps sampling every rapid page shows one. No guarantee is claimed at the coarse rate.
- Wall-clock residue: the frame loop caps each frame's simulated advance at the configured frame cap (50 ms). Driven with 120 ms wall steps, 100 frames advance the simulation 5000 ms against 12000 ms of wall time; the 7000 ms residue is an accounting property of the cap, reported here, not corrected by construction.
- Perceptual clearance during formation is not measured; clearance is priced by a geometry proxy at formation start (fraction of title targets already covered by the previous page's particles 0.138 against a 0.15 threshold; bounding-box overlap 0.253 against 0.35), and the proxy is shown to discriminate: with the release scatter zeroed the same measures read 0.197 / 0.955 and fail the overlap threshold.
- The release-scatter distance was tested at smaller values against the clearance thresholds and no smaller value passed both; the shipped scatter distance is the smallest tested value that passes, not a reduced one.
