SCORE-BLIND BUILD RECORD — T16 particle document flip, current build
(Construction facts and the build's own deterministic check results. No scores, no history.)

WHAT THE BUILD IS
A single self-contained HTML file. A stack of document pages, rendered as particles sampled from config-rendered text and shapes, flips through its page counter (pages 1 to 14 at counter checkpoints 1, 6 and 14, at 0 ms, 1500 ms and 2200 ms of a 6000 ms cycle); the finished stack disperses, a title condenses out of the particle stream with a ring above it, the title holds, then disperses inside a dedicated seam interval (5700–6000 ms) and the cycle restarts with no borrowed particles carried across the seam. Phases: flip [0,2600), release [2600,3100), form [3100,4600), hold [4600,5700), seam [5700,6000). The nominal hold ends at 5700 ms; the cycle rolls over at 6000 ms. Page intervals: 300 ms early, 87.5 ms rapid, and a 400 ms final-page interval. The build starts its own animation loop on load.

CONSTRUCTION FACTS
- Two distinct faces per page turn, on separate particle sets. The OUTGOING face is the leaving page's own line texture PLUS a dedicated edge set: the leaving page's outline targets are borrowed as first-class exiting targets, so the outgoing face carries outline + texture through the whole handoff. Both faces fold around the page's left-edge pivot on the turn profiles (outgoing factor crosses below zero at mid-window; incoming factor rises from the configured minimum to 1.0 by the window's readable onset, 70% of the effective turn window). The INCOMING face is the new page's own sampled target set (outline + header + line texture) unfolding from the pivot. The pool is sized for the concurrent demand the handoff actually creates: the incoming page, the exiting line set, two edge sets (the previous handoff's edge may still be exiting), and one vanished set still scattering, grossed up by the reserve fraction.
- Page dispatch: every page change crossed by a simulation step is dispatched in chronological order at its scheduled instant (one event order across segment entries and page changes; ties resolve segment entry first). Diagnostics keep three separate counters that cannot be conflated: schedule coverage (entries crossed), assignments (pages whose targets were actually assigned), and presented (pages that reached a drawn frame). At 120 ms callbacks, coverage equals assignments for every page of the default schedule (14/14) and of a 40-page / 6000 ms schedule (40/40); a single step crossing many pages and the flip exit assigns the identical page sequence as fine-step execution.
- Turn and registration: every page change starts its own turn window at the change's SCHEDULED instant; the effective duration derives from the interval it must fit. Registration starts at the particle's actual arrival instant (borrow start + delay + travel, evaluated at event time inside the step, not at the detecting tick), and registration completion is recorded at its event instant. A turning particle's intensity binds to its OWN registration, not to the window's end.
- Readability predicates (two, both per-particle): strict = settled, registered, and the turn window complete; geometric = settled and registered with the particle's face factor at least the configured geometric factor. The build's dwell measurements count strict-readable frames only at presentation boundaries (drawn frames). Measured on the build's own instrument (offsets 0 / 7.3 / 13.1 / 29.7 ms × pages 6–13): strict-readable presented cells 32/32 at 60 fps and 26/32 at 50 ms steps — at 50 ms steps, six named cells (offset 0 pages 9 and 13; offset 13.1 pages 8 and 12; offset 29.7 pages 7 and 11) have no presented frame inside their readable window, because the preserved rapid interval is 87.5 ms and a 50 ms draw grid can fall entirely outside a page's readable span.
- Hold accounting is measured, not scheduled, and is non-vacuous: a form's completion is reported only when its assigned target set equals its required target set AND every assigned particle has registered; an empty assignment reports UNRESOLVED — empty assignment, and a partial assignment reports UNRESOLVED, never a resolved hold. At the defaults, joint title-and-ring registration completes at the 4600 ms hold entry on the event-time basis, and the observed resolved hold is the full nominal 1100 ms. Both hold events are named in diagnostics: nominal hold end 5700 ms, cycle rollover 6000 ms.
- Feasibility is a single gate with exactly two outcomes: FEASIBLE (the resolved configuration applies whole) or REFUSED ATOMICALLY (the candidate is rejected as a unit, the refusal with its reasons is written to an observable rejected-configurations record, the previously applied configuration remains in force, and a refused mid-run replacement preserves the live run — no restart rewind). The gate decides on a conservative PRE-COMMIT lifecycle bound computed before any execution: per-interval schedule lifecycle, formation lifecycle, complete page coverage, positive intervals, the intensity hierarchy with its minimum separation, non-empty target sets, magnitude bounds, placed-box containment (below), the fit condensation floor (below), and the FINAL-PALETTE contrast condition: after sanitisation resolves the candidate, the gate re-evaluates the exact contrast pairs and floors sanitisation applies (bright colour ≥ 4, dim colour ≥ 2, ring colour ≥ 2 against the resolved background) PLUS the DOM colours (counter, counter label, title label against the resolved background), and refuses on any failure. A candidate background that collides with the default foreground (contrast 1:1) is refused by the gate.
- Validation trust: the validation schema and the trusted defaults live in frozen structures outside the editable configuration object; fallbacks read the frozen copy, so editing the exposed defaults object cannot rewrite a fallback, and the gate's magnitude bound, contrast floors, and separation minimum are schema constants, not configuration keys. The direct-edit corpus (each field class corrupted in source: numeric → string / non-finite / oversized; a colour → a contrast-colliding value; a group → null; the trusted defaults themselves → mutation attempt) asserts the independent fallback or the gate refusal fires on both the direct-edit and the URL-override paths.
- Shared values are single-sourced: one resolved onset fraction of the turn window is read by the schedule's lifecycle bound, by the registration cap, and by both face profiles — an onset swap (0.5, 0.85) moves all four together on both input routes. Initial particle colours derive from the resolved palette. The fitted title's horizontal centre is a resolved configuration value consumed by both sampling and fit.
- Fit and placement are one construction: fitting returns the placed ink box (the extents as drawn, with the fit's own alignment and baseline); the gate constrains the placed box to its region (a header offset that places the ink at the page's bottom edge is refused) and refuses a fit condensed below the sampling floor instead of silently thinning. The counter group is measured with the label's own font settings including its letter spacing, and the above-stack fallback records its actual clearance against the stack's placed box.
- Pacing: the main loop accumulates elapsed time and advances in bounded sub-steps; one callback may advance at most a configured multiple (4×) of the smallest schedule interval, and any excess is carried as named dropped time in diagnostics — reported, never silently simulated in one draw or silently discarded. 100 callbacks of 120 ms advance exactly 12000 ms of simulation with no residue and no dropped time. Capture and release evaluate position and velocity at the actual boundary instant: a capture's origin and velocity snapshot are taken at the boundary, and a release's scatter is constructed from the rendered state at its boundary.
- Restart: a restart resets the complete run state — simulation time, phase, page, trace, transitions, diagnostics (including frame statistics, the page counters, and dropped time), the frame-time accumulator, turn-factor outputs, DOM opacity, and the particle pool (re-seeded). A deliberately dirtied run (queued frame time, mid-turn factors, faded DOM) restarted and then driven with an identical timestamp sequence matches a fresh load exactly, including its first callback's advance. An exit that has completed by a later change's scheduled instant is released at that instant, never re-exited; an in-flight exit's clock is never reset.
- Release: a released page set holds its settled intensity until the clearance point of its scatter window, then drops to surplus intensity — the silhouette clears geometrically before its intensity falls.

STATED LIMITATIONS
- PIXEL-UNVERIFIED: no rendered-pixel verification route exists for this build; every measurement above is a deterministic source-model measurement (a stubbed rasterizer and the build's own predicates), not a rendered frame.
- Rapid-page dwell: the preserved catalogue anchors (87.5 ms rapid intervals; checkpoints at 0 / 1500 / 2200 ms) cap each rapid page's presented dwell; no construction inside those anchors gives every rapid page a long strict dwell, and the build does not claim one — it measures and reports presented dwell per page instead.
- The fold's depth character beyond the preserved interval structure is as built; the build makes no claim about rendered perception of the turn.
