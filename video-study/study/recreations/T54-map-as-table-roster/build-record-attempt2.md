## §2 Construction (neutral facts)
- One self-contained HTML file; inline SVG: ground rect + one generated circle per abstract state blob (centre/radius anchors in CONFIG — generated arrangement, not traced boundaries) inside a single camera group; the camera is the SVG viewBox window computed from a keyframe track.
- CONFIG-first: 69 top-level keys (roster, state anchors, palette, type sizes + a glyph-width table, camera primitives, pass regions, layout and search parameters, full-plate style tokens). The run duration is DERIVED, never stored: duration = passStart + pass + return move + final hold, where passStart = max(specimen full-dwell end, placement end + post-place hold) under a time rescale proportional to the placement span — a longer or slower roster re-derives a consistent timeline instead of truncating.
- Timeline (default roster): camera opens on the Midwest zoom (scale 1.9) and holds while that region's chips place; an interpolated move reaches the full view at 3000 ms; the full map holds 3000–6000 ms while remaining chips place in full view (placement completes 4400 ms); a roster pass then visits five regions (west · central · midwest · south · northeast), each framed from the laid-out positions of its chips at a fitted scale, showing that region's name plates in two waves; an interpolated return reaches the full view again and the completed map holds with its count plate to the derived end (14000 ms).
- Chips are generated initials discs (never portraits). Same-state positions are generated by a deterministic golden-angle spiral (no repetition at any count, capacity validated). A deterministic relaxation pass separates all disc pairs to at least the configured centre distance; any chip displaced beyond the configured threshold from its home anchor carries a thin connector line to that anchor (the specimen's own Northeast device, §1).
- Name plates are positioned by a bounded backtracking search against the CURRENT camera window: plates stay inside the window's intersection with the map, respect a reserved strip when the full-roster plate is up, never overlap each other, and never cover a visible chip's Elect tag. In the opening zoom, plates show for the most recent regional chips (featured-plate policy, as in the segment's zoom state); the roster pass exposes every identity exactly in its region's waves.
- Elect tags sit on their disc (as in the segment) at a position chosen by a scored search (fixed candidates + a deterministic grid) that never covers another chip's centre and never overlaps another tag.
- Validation runs before any DOM construction and rejects, with field-specific errors: duplicate or unknown state ids (roster, opening region, pass regions), chips referencing unknown states, per-state capacity overflow, pass-region coverage gaps, nonpositive durations, out-of-range ratios, and opening-region readiness failures (a region chip placing after its camera beat, or outside its window).
- The pop animation's effective duration is the configured pop scaled against the placement spacing, so pops never overlap the cadence at any spacing. Initials are Unicode-aware (an optional per-chip initials field overrides; a configured fallback covers letterless names); over-wide initials are clamped to a configured maximum width via textLength.
