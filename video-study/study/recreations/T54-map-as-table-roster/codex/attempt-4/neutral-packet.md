# T54 — Map-as-table roster · neutral facts packet
Technique: a flat-color US map serves as the table; each member of an electorate is a circular chip placed on their home state with a name plate and, where relevant, an "Elect" tag; the camera zooms to the region under discussion; the full map appears when the roster completes. Packet evidence window: the technique runs 1:30–throughout the source video.

## Opening evidence — two samplings of the same frames
- Coarse sampling (1 fps sub-beat table; 2 fps re-observation): Midwest regional zoom 0–3 s → full map 3–6 s → pan away 6–7 s. On this sampling: regional hold ends ~2100 ms, full arrival 3000 ms, full dwell 3000–6000 ms.
- Frame-granularity sampling (5 fps frames from the segment): regional framing at 1.0 s · widening underway at 1.2 s · near-full at 1.6 s · full map settled at 2.0 s · full still framed at 6.0/6.2 s · pan-away underway by 6.4 s. On this sampling: hold ends 1100 ms, move 1100–2000 ms, full arrival 2000 ms, full dwell 2000–6000 ms.
- The pan-away 6000–7000 ms is common to both samplings and is not in dispute.
- **This build is constructed on the frame-granularity timeline (basis B): hold-end 1100 ms · arrival 2000 ms · full dwell to 6000 ms · pan-away 6000–7000 ms.**

## Construction facts
- One self-contained HTML file; inline SVG: ground rect + one generated circle per abstract state blob (centre/radius anchors in CONFIG — generated arrangement, not traced boundaries) inside a single camera group; the camera is the SVG viewBox window computed from a keyframe track. A separate foreground group, drawn after all chip groups, carries every name plate, Elect tag and plate leader line.
- CONFIG-first: 81 top-level keys (roster, state anchors, palette, type sizes + a glyph-metrics table bound to the configured font family and weight, camera primitives, pass regions, layout and search parameters, full-plate style tokens). All plate, tag and initials widths are computed from the single glyph-metrics table. The run duration is DERIVED, never stored.
- On load the file starts its own clock through its internal play path (a CONFIG boolean controls this; the external seek/play/pause/render API is preserved for the comparison surface, which becomes a control rather than the only starter). With the boolean off, the piece waits at t=0 for an external caller — a named limitation of that configuration.
- Timeline as built (default roster): regional hold 0–1100 ms; interpolated move 1100–2000 ms; full map 2000–6000 ms; interpolated pan-away 6000–7000 ms (camera centre shifted by the configured exit shift, map leaving the frame); then a roster pass over five regions as a separately identified completion demonstration; then an interpolated return to the full view and a final full hold. Derived total duration: 84550 ms. The placement lifecycle across the pass and the final hold is a labelled allocation model: the segment window shows an already-built roster, while the source video's build-up runs 1:30–throughout.
- Placement: each chip's placement instant is DERIVED from the camera track and its final laid geometry — a chip places at a stationary instant whose camera window contains its full extent (disc + Elect tag rect). Six opening-region chips place inside the opening hold at a derived 140 ms step; the other 49 place inside the full dwell at a derived ≈77.6 ms step; no placement occurs during any camera move. Every pop runs the full configured 120 ms (pops may overlap; measured maximum concurrency 2 against a configured cap of 4). Last placement + pop settle = 5922 ms, before the 6000 ms pan start; the completed state is the state the pan-away leaves and the final hold returns to. The full-roster count plate first appears at placement end + pop settle, is hidden while the camera is away from the full view, and returns for the final hold. Its reserved strip is computed in code from its style tokens with an explicit px→unit conversion (38 units at the 360 px reference stage; the overlay's CSS line-height is set from the same factor the reserve consumes).
- Roster pass: each region is visited in wave groups of at most four members; the camera frames each group (group bounds include Elect tag extents) and holds while that group's name plates show. Each wave's hold is computed from a per-name reading budget: base + 400 ms per name + a per-longest-character term, with the floor derived from the same budget (one name × 400 ms). Wave membership is driven by stationary-elapsed time, which accrues only while the camera is stationary. Name plates are hidden whenever the camera is moving; at a stationary beat each plate takes a collision-free position from an obstacle-scored candidate search (obstacles: every placed chip's disc and every Elect tag), filtered to candidates whose centre lies within a configured displacement cap (240 units) of the owner chip; whenever a displayed plate's centre is displaced beyond the connector threshold from its chip, a thin leader line joins plate edge to chip edge, its endpoints a pure function of the plate rect and the chip. A plate with no collision-free in-cap position at a beat is hidden at that beat; a second-chance pass lets a hidden plate take a pocket from an already-placed plate when every displaced plate re-places against the updated set.
- Final hold: at the full view, name plates cycle in width-balanced index groups of at most five members (plate widths dealt round-robin across the groups, so no beat carries several of the widest plates); each group's step is derived per group (base + 400 ms × names in that group) and the final hold is the sum of the group steps (25300 ms on the default roster), so coverage and duration cannot diverge on a resized roster: a 56-chip roster derives 12 groups whose last group starts and ends inside the derived duration.
- Chips are generated initials discs (never portraits), radius 9 units. Same-state positions are generated by a deterministic golden-angle spiral. A deterministic relaxation pass separates all disc pairs to at least the configured centre distance (measured minimum 22.0 units); any chip displaced beyond the configured threshold from its home anchor carries a thin connector line to that anchor, drawn outside the pop-scaled group; connector endpoints are a pure function of anchor and laid position. Initials are extracted as grapheme clusters: combining marks are retained inside words, the no-Segmenter fallback groups trailing combining marks onto the preceding grapheme, casing uses the configured locale on both paths, and the configured initials count applies as one total-count policy to single- and multi-word names alike (all 55 default initials verified unchanged against the previous build's extraction).
- Elect tags are positioned by a scored search that avoids every chip's rendered initials bounds — including the tag owner's own initials — and every other tag; all ten tags hold a collision-free position and no tag covers its owner's initials (tags may still overlap disc circles at their edges, as the specimen's own tags do). Name plates at stationary beats are verified pairwise disjoint and disjoint from every placed disc, initials box and tag, inside the camera window, within the displacement cap, with a leader whose endpoints match its chip when displaced.
- Validation runs before any DOM construction in three phases with early return: a pure schema phase type/finite/range-checks every CONFIG leaf (grid steps, dwell and index parameters, text-metrics values and full-plate tokens included); a cross-field phase checks ordering relations, the font profile binding (family and weight), and glyph coverage — every character of every rendered string (names, labels, the tag word, the count label after substitution, the fallback initials) must exist in the glyph table, else a field-specific error names the character and its source string; a geometry phase runs layout, timeline and the feasibility gate only when the first two phases are clean. The browser init path throws on errors before DOM construction.
- A layout-feasibility result (minimum centre distance, tag fallbacks, tag-vs-owner-initials, displayed-plate collisions, displacement/leader census, identity exposure coverage) is computed for the final layout and reported as validation errors when it fails; on the default roster it passes with every identity displayed at one or more stationary beats (55/55).
