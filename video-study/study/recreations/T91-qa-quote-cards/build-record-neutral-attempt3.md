### List replacement, validation, fit contract
- **Replacement:** an override carrying `cardsReplace: [...]` — or a merged `schedule.replaceCards: true` together with a `cards` array — replaces the card list wholesale; the default per-index patch behaviour is unchanged otherwise. Replaced cards that omit `slotMs`/`revealSpanMs` receive derived values (reveal span from schedule.defaultRevealSpanMs; slot = the larger of the card's reading interval at the configured rate and reveal + minimum hold + exit), and each derivation is recorded as a warning. Under replacement, a specimen selection that the override does not explicitly supply derives to the last card of the replaced list, also recorded as a warning; an explicitly supplied out-of-range index is a validation error. An empty list under replacement semantics is invalid (validation error), never a silent restore and never a blank stage.
- **Validation before first paint:** every CONFIG group must be a non-null object of the expected shape; every numeric leaf in every group and every card must be a finite number inside its printed domain (timings are integers inside a practical cap; the second-line fraction lies in (0, 1]; line heights lie in [1, 5]); card fields must be non-empty strings with integer years; palette values must be hex; the configured regions must sit inside their containers at the resolved geometry (checked at the stage width and at the minimum width). Any violation renders a visible error state naming the offending key/index and the expectation, exposes the list on window.__t91.errors, and renders no schedule. Unknown keys at the top level, inside any group, and inside card objects are collected as warnings: the warnings render on the error panel when errors exist, and on a warning strip at the stage foot (outside the card and HUD bounds) when the schedule renders; window.__t91.warnings is the programmatic mirror.
- **Fit contract (deterministic, ordered):** shrink the quote size in fit.stepPx steps, clamped at the fit.floorPx floor, until the quote block fits the quote zone in height and every wrapped line fits the quote width; the shrink loop is bounded by fit.maxSteps. If the block still exceeds the zone at the floor, or a single word still exceeds the quote width at the floor, reject naming the card, the word where applicable, and the overflow in px — never silent clipping, never overlap with the attribution. Speaker exactly 1 line and no speaker token wider than the attribution width; role at most fit.roleMaxLines lines, no role token wider than the attribution width, and the attribution block inside geom.attrH. Fitting and rendering share one font-metric basis: in the browser, canvas measurements in the rendered font string (its style taken from CONFIG per text kind); in the harness, the PIL stand-in tables for the same stack.

### Viewport-derived geometry and resize

At 360 the factor is 1; at 320 the factor is 0.889 (card 328→291.6, quote width 256→227.6, attribution text width 274→243.6). Viewports below stage.minWidth (320) produce the error state naming the minimum. A resize path recomputes geometry, fit, and reveal line counts for the new width and re-renders at the current schedule time — the clock does not restart, and a width change mid-reveal re-derives the visible-line count from the new wrap at the same local time; resizing back to a supported width restores the schedule display. geom.attrH is 104, sized for the worst attribution case across both widths; the quote zone re-derives to 226px and every settled quote fits inside it at both widths with ≥67.6px spare.
