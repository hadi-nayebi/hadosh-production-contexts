**What changed (QC checklist v2.21, `files/qc-checklist.md`):**
- S-10's ≥70% figures are now labelled **house targets**, not platform benchmarks — YouTube publishes no universal retention threshold; its guidance is comparison against your own channel's history.
- **S-13 Evidence coverage:** each scoreboard round sweeps published pieces against scoreboard files; a missing scoreboard is named *evidence debt* (founding debts: HLjOhOkhkkY — none; the Lesson 1 pair — partial snapshot only).
- **S-14 Re-read ladder:** an unjudgeable 48 h scoreboard is re-read at 7 d and 28 d before it may close as "unjudgeable at this scale."
- **S-15 Pooled batch judgment:** when no single piece is judgeable, judge the batch pooled (summed views/watch time, channel-aggregate AVD / % viewed) — distribution and channel patterns only, never beat-level creative claims.
- **S-16 Anchor class:** a re-judged film's analytics anchor distribution facts only, never creative floors — HLjOhOkhkkY was re-judged on quality (2026-10-29), so its numbers, when read, describe what the feed did with a rejected film, not what works.

**Why it matters for production (including long-form):** retention evidence is now collected on a ladder and judged at the scale the data can actually support, instead of being closed at 48 h with a verdict the sample never earned. If you run scoreboards on the long-form side, the same four rules travel: label your targets as yours, sweep for missing scoreboards, re-read before closing, and never let a re-judged piece's numbers set a creative floor.
