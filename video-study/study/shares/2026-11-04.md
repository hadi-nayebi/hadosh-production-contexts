# Study share — 2026-11-04 — Topic 8: Community & social formats

**What was studied:** the community-post record in trust order — first the standard written after the 2026-10-28 text-only failure (verified: it exists in the 1M goal's files, and the DIY goal's community-post checklist does cite it), then the pieces that learned format facts the hard way (the AGI poll package, the daily bank, the community job's Cycle 4 record), then YouTube Help's own post pages in full, then creator guides for the points where they agree. Full note: `hidden_files/study/notes/2026-11-04-community-social-formats.md`.

**What the record actually says:** the core standard was written and did become the checklist's source — but the format facts never joined it. No governing file carried a single platform limit: not the text poll's 65 characters per option, not the image poll's 36-character captions (the cap that truncated our live poll's option mid-word), not the quiz's 80/30-character answers, one correct answer, 500-character explanation, or the fact that a saved quiz cannot be edited at all. Those numbers lived only inside individual post packages. Worse, the standard's own format variable still read "promote → video post" six days after its own amendment had replaced that rule for Shorts — a builder reading the standard alone would rebuild the pillarboxed post the amendment exists to prevent. And since 2026-11-02 the channel runs two lanes — a scored lane checked by Hadi, and a daily-basic lane publishing under his standing authorization, some of it in deliberate text form — while the governing files still banned text-only posts outright and mentioned the score gate (5 or more earns iteration; below 5 is signal only) nowhere at all.

**What changed:**
- **The community-post standard** (1M goal files): the format variable corrected in place to its own amendment's rule; the timing/check variable scoped to the two lanes; a new **Format table** giving every native format — text, image/GIF (up to 10 images), video, playlist, text poll, image poll, quiz, scheduling — with its platform limits and our decided use for each; and a new **Lanes & disposition** section carrying the score gate and the daily-basic lane's scoped forms.
- **The community-post checklist** (DIY goal files) grew from 25 to **29 boxes**: the format and check boxes are scoped to their lanes, and four new boxes require the mechanical format counts, quiz integrity (one correct answer, the explanation carrying the teach point, proofread-as-final because a saved quiz cannot be edited), multi-image order (first image carries the claim), and a recorded lane + score disposition for every post.
- Exercised, not just written: our AGI image poll passes the new counts (captions 27 and 23 of 36); the daily bank's quiz passes its limits but exposed one real gap — its quiz explanation is recorded nowhere, which the new quiz box now catches; the bank's text post passes under its lane's scope, while the same form in the scored lane still fails, as it should.

**Why it matters for production (including long-form):** a community post is a small artifact with hard platform edges, and the edges are now in the governing files instead of in the memory of whoever built the last package. The durable lesson for long-form too: when a later ruling supersedes a standing variable, correct the variable itself in the same pass — an amendment appended at the end of the file retires nothing, and the next builder reads the list, not the footnotes.
