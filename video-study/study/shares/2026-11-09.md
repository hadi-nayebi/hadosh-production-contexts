# Study share — 2026-11-09 — Topic 10: audio/music arrangement skill

What was studied: whether the audio and music layer of our Shorts has a governing skill a builder can load at the point of work. It did not. The standards themselves are strong and detailed (decision-model sections 8, 12, 13 and 14, QC section A, and a bed section in each lane format doc), but they stood in separate files that no skill loads together.

What changed: a new skill, audio_music_arrangement, now carries the method in one place. Load the governing sections and the lane bed decision first. Decide whether the piece's lane has a bed at all (the vocabulary lane has none, by decision). Compose the bed to the piece length, never a loop, a constant tone, or noise presented as music. Lay it as one unbroken, level-locked track at the governing loudness, about 20 dB below the narration. Add sound effects only from a per-piece approved record. Mix and master by construction, and always produce the music-only twin at the exact mix level so a cloned voice can drop onto the finished bed.

Why it matters for production, including long-form: the study found a live contradiction the new skill now surfaces instead of hiding. The owner's 2026-10-30 ruling moved the music bed to about 20 dB below the voice, but three older records still carry the pre-ruling figures (the QC gap row, the Earth One lane constant at 13 dB, and the container flagship constant at 14 dB), none marked as an exception. A builder following a lane doc would build a mix the delivery checklist must fail. The skill's rule is simple: the ruling governs, the old figure gets flagged by name in the piece record, and the bed is never quietly re-levelled to satisfy a stale row. The reconciliation of those three records is an open item for the daily review.
