# Study share — 2026-11-11 — Topic 11: Cinematic 3D Journey

**What was studied.** The journey style Hadi had Archer study on 2026-10-27: Charles Zheng's reel following one photo across the Pacific in about 200 milliseconds — a polished 3D world the camera flies through, one thing transforming end-to-end (photo, bits, 1,500 numbered packets, light, photo again), a persistent millisecond clock as the spine, big numbers acting, labels riding their objects, and physical scale jumps doing the work of scene changes. Read back from the filed category record and the reel's own lookup record, with canon and the decision model read for the join points, motivated-camera craft for the movement rule, and Zacks' event-segmentation research for why scale changes work as boundaries.

**The finding.** The style had been studied in full and filed — and joined to nothing. At the session's first probe it scored zero citations in canon, in the decision model and in QC, and the decision model's entire camera coverage was one fixed-frame row. Fifteen days of presence, no conversion.

**What changed.** Two files, the two homes the curriculum names for this topic. The decision model gained section 10E, ten rows that make the journey plannable: a lane gate (the form only where the teaching is itself a journey, and a true-3D build still enters specimen-first on Hadi's word — the rows plan the style, they do not open the lane), the through-line object with its transformation chain written before build, camera path as narrative with a named story reason for every flight segment, the scale ladder as the scene-switch table, dwell at payoff beats as an emphasis budget spent by camera speed, the HUD counter measuring the piece's own quantity, numbers as actors with labels on objects, the lane palette declared with one style per piece, the never-frozen frame, and a journey audit on the delivered file. Canon section 7 now names the category file in its reference hierarchy as the second lane's style source — explicitly not a source for the scenes-and-words style. Two reconciliations are written into both places: the reel's bottom captions are not adopted (Hadi's no-captions law governs), and its navy-and-gold palette is a separate declared lane palette, never mixed into a chalk piece.

**Why it matters for production.** When the next Earth One or workshop piece is planned as a journey, its camera reasons, transformation chain, scale ladder and spine are decided in the plan against standing rows, instead of the planner needing to know that a category file from October exists. The 2D-stealable principles — follow one thing, a growing counter as spine, numbers as actors, labels attached to moving elements — are also recorded in the new section as borrowable by ordinary pieces without pretending to switch lanes.
