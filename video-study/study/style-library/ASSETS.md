### AS-01 — Energy by density, not by cuts (pacing principle)
- Lesson: a piece can feel fast with almost no cuts — this specimen runs 25 hard cuts in 198 s and one 22.5 s zero-cut stretch, yet never feels slow, because intra-shot animation density carries the energy. Cut rate is the wrong tempo dial; animated-elements-per-second is the right one.
- Evidence: P1 digest.md §4 (tempo map) + T17 — consumer: all production (pacing decisions in Plan).

### AS-02 — The title built from the work itself (storytelling device)
- Lesson: the spectacle title is assembled out of the artifact the video is about (thumbnails → "LOOK"; lit windows → the title) — the claim "look what I can do" is proven by its own letterforms. A title that is evidence, not decoration.
- Evidence: P1 T09 (0:50–0:53), T20 (2:37–2:50) — consumer: openings, thumbnails.
