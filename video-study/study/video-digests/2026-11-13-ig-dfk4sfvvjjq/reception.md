## Reception lesson

This piece's reception was driven by spread, not by its base: at ~6.4 likes per follower across two captures, with the follower count itself still climbing 8% in half a day four days after posting, the audience that responded was mostly not the audience the creator already had — and what that wider audience did in the comments was re-perform the piece (quoting its punchlines, declaring replays, demanding the song off-platform and the process behind it) far more than it evaluated the piece, while the only sustained opposition gathered under the single "AI slop" frame.
