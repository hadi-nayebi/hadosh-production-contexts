## Reception lesson

The reception this record can actually price is a mirror's, and it teaches a mirror's lesson: an 11-year-old brand film, hosted on a small regional channel it was never made for, stands at ≈40× that channel's current subscriber count (73,574 lifetime views against 1,830 subscribers, capture 2026-11-16 — a scale comparison between two counts, not a count of viewers) while converting very little of its view count into visible interaction (0.49% likes per view, 9 comments in a decade). Inside that thin comment record, the responses that earned the most agreement are the film's participatory test working as designed — viewers reporting, with evident pleasure, that they could keep up (joint-most-liked, 10 likes) — and the sample's only two moment references (0:36 · 0:37) coincide with the mirror heatmap's peak stretch (34.8–37.8 s), while relative replay intensity falls to near-zero over the closing ~15 s. The canonical film's own reception remains unpriced: its counts were not retrieved, and no figure in this record may be read in their place.
