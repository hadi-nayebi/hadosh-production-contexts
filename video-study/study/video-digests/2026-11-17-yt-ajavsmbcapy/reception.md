## Reception lesson

The audience for this explainer does not behave like students of a mechanism; it behaves like partisans arriving with a verdict (abolish 33 vs defend 13 in the frozen sample), re-framing a 2020 film through the 2024 cycle (17), returning to it on an election calendar (6), and auditing its checkable claims in public (7) — while a real minority (13) leaves still asking how electors are chosen, which is the unmet teaching need the reception actually evidences. For our own explainers the priced lesson is that a contested-mechanism piece is received as an argument first and a lesson second: its reception is dominated by position-taking, its corrections land on its most checkable claims, and its durable value shows up as seasonal re-use — none of which is visible in the headline counts (8.40M views at 0.66 views per capture-time subscriber, still moving +334 views in a same-day 6-hour window six years after posting).
