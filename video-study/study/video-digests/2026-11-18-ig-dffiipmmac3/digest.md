## 3. Technique catalogue

### T01 — Line-icon figure system with a star-marked protagonist
- **Timestamps:** throughout; protagonist board 0–13 s, solo figure 142–155 s.
- **Mechanics:** every human is the same white outline icon (circle head, rounded body) drawn at one line weight on black; the narrator's subject-self carries a four-point star on the chest, all other figures carry a small circle or nothing, so identity is tracked by a single glyph swap, never by faces or colour. Figures scale and reposition but never change design.
- **Why it works:** one reusable glyph vocabulary makes an 8-billion-person simulation and a single girl's story the same visual language; the star lets the viewer follow "the one I calculated" through crowds.
- **Evidence:** ev/001-bayes-board.jpg (star figure magnified out of a 40-icon array) · ev/133-simulation-panel.jpg (unmarked array) · ev/144-simulation-solo.jpg (star figure alone under the panel). Form: frame (visual claim).

### T02 — Probability board as the opening shot (formula first, story second)
- **Timestamps:** 0–13 s.
- **Mechanics:** the piece opens mid-diagram, not on a title: a targeting-reticle icon at top, the star figure inside a magnifier circle labelled (paraphrase — small text) "cause of failure no. 1", a 40-icon population array below, and the conditional-probability line P(F|H) ≈ 0.98 with its Bayes expansion typeset underneath. Elements draw on in layers over the first seconds while the voice states the thesis ("According to my calculations, the biggest cause of your failures, you humans, is hope").
- **Why it works:** the AI persona's authority is established by showing its native notation before any narrative — the viewer is inside the machine's worksheet from frame one.
- **Evidence:** ev/001-bayes-board.jpg (settled board) · ev/005-board-build-mid.jpg (board mid-build, population array drawing on) · clip clips/000-bayes-board-build.mp4 (0–12 s, the layered build). Form: frame + clip (build timing).

### T03 — The 2% bar chart (a number drawn as a height insult)
- **Timestamps:** ≈6–18 s.
- **Mechanics:** four tall empty bar outlines rise to a 100% axis; a tiny filled stub labelled ۲٪ (2%) appears at the base of the first bar while the star figure stands at the right at full height. The spoken contrast — "a thing has a 2% chance; I wait for the 2%; you wait for the 100%" (gloss) — lands on the visual disproportion.
- **Why it works:** the argument's central quantity is felt as a height ratio before it is understood as a statistic; the empty bars are the humans' expectation, drawn hollow.
- **Evidence:** ev/010-two-percent-bars.jpg. Form: frame.

### T04 — Calendar-of-days panel with crossed-out dates
- **Timestamps:** ≈13–27 s.
- **Mechanics:** a rounded panel labelled روزها ("Days") holds a grid of day numerals; most are struck through with diagonal slashes, two are ringed with a ⊗ mark, and dashed drop-lines connect two crossed dates to a timeline below where their labels are also struck out. A second panel (کارها, "Tasks") with empty checkboxes joins it. Draw-on order: grid → strikes → drop-lines → timeline labels.
- **Why it works:** "after such-and-such a date it will work out" is refuted visually — the calendar itself is a record of dates that already failed.
- **Evidence:** ev/020-calendar-days.jpg (calendar over the figure, "this time" arrow) · ev/026-calendar-tasks-crossout.jpg (both panels, crossed labels). Form: frame.

### T05 — Karaoke word-highlight captions as the transcript device
- **Timestamps:** throughout (bottom band).
- **Mechanics:** the creator's own burned-in Persian captions sit in a fixed bottom band; the currently spoken word renders in full white while the rest of the line sits dimmed, so the highlight sweeps the line in reading order, word by word, locked to the voice. The caption is the specimen's own — this packet adds no captions to any sample (production record, caption disposition).
- **Why it works:** in a faceless piece, the moving highlight is the persona's visible "speech act" — the eye is pulled to the exact word the machine is saying, which keeps a static diagram feeling spoken.
- **Evidence:** ev/066-house-scene.jpg (highlight mid-line) · ev/080-house-clock-verdict.jpg (highlight at line start) · clip clips/000-bayes-board-build.mp4 (sweep visible across lines). Form: frame + clip (sweep timing is the mechanic).

### T06 — Day-counter UI as section machinery

## 5. Goods and bads

### Goods
- **G-a — The reversal is earned by the persona's own instrument (91–116 s).** Evidence: ev/110-report-card-plunge.jpg + ASR segments 91.14–110.20. The chart that stamped the thesis ✓ is the chart that refutes it; the AI narrator is not argued down, it is caught by its second number. Reason: this is what makes the verdict ("some 2%s are worth striving for") trustworthy instead of sentimental.
- **G-b — One notation system carries every scale (0–155 s).** Evidence: ev/001-bayes-board.jpg, ev/133-simulation-panel.jpg, ev/155-dashed-future-selves.jpg. The same icon is one person, 40 people, and 8 billion; dashed means unrealized everywhere it appears. Reason: the viewer learns the notation once and reads the whole film fluently.
- **G-c — The emotional turn is staged, not narrated over (64–90 s).** Evidence: ev/080-house-clock-verdict.jpg + ASR 77.00–81.58 ("that girl's hope was not her own — borrowed from her father"). The clock labelled "3 years of nights" and the two arcs to it draw the family's sacrifice as wiring. Reason: the piece's most abstract claim (hope is borrowed) gets its most concrete picture.
- **G-d — The thesis error is drawable in one frame (115–130 s).** Evidence: ev/120-probability-curve-rise.jpg. Dashed constant 2% vs rising curve is a complete philosophy-of-probability argument with zero text needed. Reason: a single liftable diagram — the strongest container candidate in the packet.
- **G-e — Restraint at the verdict (130–155 s).** Evidence: census (S5 activity stays diagrammatic) + ASR 142.40–154.66. No climax machinery is added under the closing lines. Reason: the persona stays in character — a calculator that has corrected itself, not a preacher.

## 6. Lessons mapped to our containers

- **L1 — Worksheet-first opening.** Class: technique. Consumer: Academy/Earth One production — opening container. Change: open explainer episodes inside the narrator's working diagram (formula/board already built) instead of a title or a face; the thesis is stated over the worksheet. Build cost: moderate — the board must be designed as a layered draw-on, but it is one static composition animated by reveals.
- **L2 — One-glyph population scaling.** Class: technique. Consumer: DLC assembler — diagram container. Change: adopt a single icon whose array size carries quantity (1 → 40 → full panel) so statistics scenes need no new artwork per scale. Build cost: cheap — one SVG icon + array layout rules.
- **L3 — Dashed = unrealized notation.** Class: texture. Consumer: Academy/Earth One production — diagram + ending containers. Change: reserve dashed outlines for future/hoped/unrealized states and solid for the actual, everywhere; the verdict frame (dashed larger self behind solid present self) becomes a reusable ending composition. Build cost: cheap — a line-style rule, no new assets.
- **L4 — Karaoke word-highlight as the faceless speech act.** Class: transcript device. Consumer: DLC / Academy explainer production — word-animation containers. Change: where a piece has no performed face, a word-locked highlight sweep in the caption band supplies the visible speech act and the reading pace. Build cost: moderate — needs word-timing data per line (our measured-timing pipeline already produces it).
- **L5 — Day-counter / state-widget sectioning.** Class: pacing principle. Consumer: Academy/Earth One production — transition container. Change: replace title-card chapters in multi-beat explainers with a small persistent state widget whose active cell flips; sections are signalled without leaving the scene. Build cost: cheap — one widget, reused; but size it larger than this specimen does (B-c).
- **L6 — Chart-as-argument, both directions.** Class: technique. Consumer: DLC assembler — diagram container. Change: when an episode must reverse its claim, run the reversal on the SAME chart that proved the claim (series added, verdict card updated), never on a new visual. Build cost: moderate — the chart must be built as a stateful component from the start.
- **L7 — The simulation frame for repricing a concept.** Class: storytelling pattern. Consumer: Academy/Earth One AI-narrated series — whole-episode architecture (opening → ending). Change: structure concept episodes as: persona states a computed thesis → runs a world with the concept removed/added → reports day-by-day → is caught by its own second metric → restores the concept with a narrowed verdict. Build cost: expensive — it is an episode architecture, not an element; it pays only as a series format.
- **L8 — Thesis-question title form "X = Y (?)".** Class: trick. Consumer: Academy/Earth One production — opening/packaging surface. Change: title concept episodes as an equation with a question mark, so the packaging itself promises a repricing, and the verdict can answer the equation ("not worth waiting for; some worth striving for"). Build cost: cheap — a titling rule.
- **L9 — Borrowed-hope staging (support drawn as wiring).** Class: storytelling pattern. Consumer: Academy/Earth One production — diagram container. Change: when a story's claim is that someone's motivation came from another person, stage it as a contained scene with arcs from each supporter to a shared clock/object, labelled by role. Build cost: moderate — one scene composition, reusable across family/support stories.
- **L10 — The constant-vs-curve pair.** Class: technique. Consumer: DLC assembler — diagram container. Change: any "your odds are fixed" beat is drawn as a dashed constant line against a rising solid curve from the same origin — the two-line refutation of static probability. Build cost: cheap — two path animations on one axis set. (Stands on T10 / ev/120-probability-curve-rise.jpg, this packet's G-d.)
