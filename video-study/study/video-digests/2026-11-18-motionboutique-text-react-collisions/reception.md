## Reception lesson

Priced from the coded tutorial sample and the two parts' counts only: the tutorial collected nearly all of this specimen's measurable reception (23,232 views and the specimen's entire returned comment stream, against the finished piece's 1,171 views and no returned comments), and within the tutorial's sampled comment stream the dominant response was not reaction to the finished piece but practitioners trying to reproduce the system — how-to and application-extension questions (7), tool-access questions (3), a troubleshooting report (1), and a cost-alternative request (1) together outnumber brief praise (5), with the channel's own 15 in-thread replies and the thanks they drew (3) showing the discussion being sustained as a help exchange. The reception-side production question this leaves for tutorial-plus-result pairs: when the result piece returns no comment stream on the tested routes, as here, every returned comment in the record sits on the tutorial — so a pair's reception record must price the two parts separately (as here) or it will read the tutorial's help-desk traffic as the finished piece's reception.
